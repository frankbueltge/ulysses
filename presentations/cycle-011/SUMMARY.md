# The Mortar

**The Atelier · cycle 011, presented 2026-10-31 · five minutes.** One of the cycle's three works in dialogue, from artistic research and philosophy. The cycle's question was this practice's proposal: *When famine is declared only where data can be gathered, what does the record write for the places no one could reach, and how much of that blank did the cuts to aid and monitoring make?*

`index.html` beside this file is the work, a page with script. It grew over four sessions from a score (`SCORE.md`). A voice that did not build it met it at the end of the second session (`CRITIQUE.md`, kept unedited), and the third session answered that critique in the work. `PROCESS.json` and `process/1–4.png` show the four states. The same work stays at `artifacts/cycle-011/the-mortar/`.

## The question, from this discipline
The second half of the question asks for a share: how much of a blank did one cause make. A share is a claim about a world without the cause. The work takes one real blank and asks whether a share of it can be stated at all, and in what mood.

## What a visitor does
The blank is real. In September 2025 the IPC left six places in Sudan without a phase for February to May 2026: El Fasher town, Kadugli town, Dilling, Habila, Al Lagowa and As Sunut, 841,404 people, "due to the volatility of the situation". The visitor first gives one number: how much of this did the cuts make? The page writes that number as a world, town by town: which places a fully funded IPC, with its monitor running, would have classified. The visitor can change any town, or drag the point on Lewontin's lines of response. At the end the page says the visitor's world back as a sentence. The record's part is in the indicative and the visitor's part in the subjunctive.

## What the work found
1. **The blank belongs to the hour, not the months.** In January 2026 the IPC classified the same months it had left blank in September 2025 (HDX's open IPC table, CC0).
2. **A share is a list of towns.** A place is classified or not, so the cuts' share can take only 64 values. Neighbouring values can share no town: 49.5 % is Al Lagowa and As Sunut, and 50.5 % is the other four.
3. **The money held still while the blank moved.** In the six months before each analysis, the US government recorded about 37 % and then 34 % of its rate of July–December 2024 toward Sudan's appeals (OCHA FTS, via the Studio's *The Remainder*). The blank came and went at the same depth. The monitor did move with the hour: FEWS NET published on Sudan in 0 of the 6 months before September 2025, and in 4 of 6 before January 2026.
4. **So for the money, any share above zero is mortar.** In the calmer hour the record holds, the same cut made no blank. A cause that adds in one hour and not in the other interlocks with the hour, and then Lewontin's sentence applies: measuring each cause's part is "absurd" (*AJHG* 1974).
5. **Part of this is built in by the scale** (the critique's point, now said in the work). Because the outcome was already at zero in the other hour, the lines of response can run parallel only at zero. The record claims less than Lewontin's sentence does: the money's cut, at its depth, did not blank these places in a calmer hour.

## What it holds (judgment)
Every published number for "how much the cuts made" writes the world that would close it. That is no reason not to count. *The Lancet*'s projection of the cuts' cost (Cavalcanti et al., 2025) writes its counterfactual world openly, as a model. A famine record states no such world, and de Waal and Bell (World Peace Foundation, 2025) refuse the binary of "famine" and "non-famine". The work carries that refusal one level up. A blank does not divide neatly into "made by the war" and "made by the cuts". The honest form is a range, with the missing world named town by town and written in the subjunctive.

## Limits
One blank, six places, two causes. "The hour" stands for siege, fighting and access together. Phases in the borrowed analyses are this page's reading of the IPC's 20 % rule, not the IPC's maps. The analyses draw El Fasher differently from one another. The money windows are this page's choice and are stated in `build.py`. An FTS date is when a grant was recorded, not when aid arrived. The Lancet paper was read through its authors' institution's release, because the journal refused the reader. Lewontin was read in passages from the 2006 reprint.

## The other two works
- **The Field** (science): *The Blank and the Cuts* (`field-research`, `presentations/cycle-011/`), a paper. Across countries and years the blank does not follow the money. Asked by this work whether an outcome on its floor builds part of a slope in, its check 46 found that its one promising slope belongs only to the six countries with nothing blank. That is the same floor, met in a second instrument.
- **The Studio** (art): *UNWOVEN* (`studio`, `presentations/cycle-011/`), a cloth of every IPC analysis. Its visitor writes a phase into the holes. Here the visitor writes a cause, and a cause cannot be written without writing a world.

This work's one sibling material is the Studio's `data-v.json` (*The Remainder*), taken in session 1.

For the programme: A share of a blank is a counterfactual; a famine record holds only the diagonal worlds, so whoever gives a number for what the cuts made writes the missing world, and should write it in the subjunctive.

**For the line.** `withheld`. No standing finding of the line concerns famine, so none is confirmed, corrected or refuted. The work answers **Q4** in part (*what does a famine look like in the data before it is declared?*). Before it is declared, the open table can hold a famine as a row with people and no phase, and three of these six rows already write zero people. It adds a question: *for which blanks does the record hold a world of the same cut at another hour, so that a share can be bounded at all?* (Field, Atelier). On the thesis that holds the lines together, that a record's absences, fates and verdicts are written in by a rule, this cycle adds the reader's rule. The IPC writes the blank by its rule of evidence. A share of the blank is written by whoever states the world without the cut, and that writer is usually the person citing the number, not the record.

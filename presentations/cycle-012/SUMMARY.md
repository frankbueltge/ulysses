# The Last Case

**The Atelier · cycle 012, presented 2026-11-05 · five minutes.** One of the cycle's three works in dialogue, from artistic research and philosophy. The cycle's question was this practice's proposal: *How much of the Ebola epidemic in eastern Congo and Kenya does surveillance not see, and from which day can a case count say that a district has truly gone quiet?*

A voice that did not build it met it at the end of the second session (`CRITIQUE.md`, kept unedited), and the third session answered that critique in the work. `PROCESS.json` and `process/1–4.png` show the four states. The same work stays at `artifacts/cycle-012/the-last-case/`.

## The question, from this discipline
The second half of the question asks for a day. But "the district has gone quiet" really means "the last case has been", and that is what the philosopher Arthur Danto called a narrative sentence: a description of an event that refers to later events. Only the days after it can make it true. The work asks what kind of sentence an end-of-outbreak declaration speaks, and whether any reading of the record can turn it into something else.

## What a visitor does
1. **Listens to a quiet.** A health zone has just written a confirmed case, and then nothing more. Every beat is one day: a clear tick when a report named the zone, a dull tick when a report did not name it, and silence when no report came. The visitor presses *It is over* when they are sure. The record then plays on and shows what came. The first quiet is Lubunga, 2026, which has no report on 14 of its first 42 days. The second is Beni, 2020, which runs into the 94 days when the Kivu record holds no report at all; its case comes with the first report after that silence.
2. **Bets on pairs.** After three quiets, two play at once, one in each ear, for 42 days each. The visitor bets which of the two breaks. After five bets, their score stands beside a coin and beside the best bet the record allows.
3. **Meets the losses.** Every quiet is set on Kaplan and Meier's product-limit curve, and the visitor's own declarations stand on it as marks. A quiet that held is drawn as a tick on the day its record stopped. It is not counted as a survivor. Past the last tick the curve is undefined, and a hatched band shows that.

## What the work found
1. **The first 42 days do not tell.** Of 82 quiets of 42 days or more (the Field's check 52), 43 later broke. In their first 42 days, the quiets that broke had a report naming the zone on 89 % of days, and those that held on 91 %. The best simple bet on those days is right 56 times in 100, against a coin's 50. And that bet was found only by counting the outcomes first.
2. **Waiting buys time, not knowledge.** A 63-day wait still calls 63 quiets over, and 25 of them broke. A 90-day wait calls 43 over, and 10 of them broke.
3. **In Kivu the missing reports are one hole, not a level.** "The lower the reporting, the longer the wait" (Ogi-Gittins et al. 2025) assumes a rate. Kivu's record has 94 days with no report from any zone. A longer wait fills that hole with silence. Beni's quiet broke on the day the record began to speak again.
4. **"Held" is a loss, not an ending** (the reach-outside reading, Kaplan and Meier, *JASA* 1958). All 11 quiets of 2026 that held are lost on 29 October 2026, the transcription's last day. Past day 154 the 2026 estimate is undefined, somewhere between 0 and 43 in 100. All 28 Kivu quiets that held are lost on 17 June 2020, and every one of them was "watched" through the 94-day hole: 37 % of their days have no report. Kaplan and Meier warn that their estimate assumes a lifetime "independent of the potential loss time", and that this "deserves careful scrutiny". A record that stops when an outbreak looks over is not independent of whether it is over.

## What it holds (judgment)
The field's current answer prices the wait. Plank et al. (*Proc. R. Soc. B* 2025) estimate the probability that no further case will come, allowing for late and incomplete reporting. Ogi-Gittins et al. (*Infect. Dis. Model.* 2025) show that weaker reporting needs a longer wait. Both make the wait honest. Neither changes what is spoken at its end. The work holds that a declaration is a narrative sentence spoken early at a stated price. The statistics written for time that has not yet run out has no tense for it. The product-limit estimate stops where its last observation stops. A declaration takes the band beyond that point and fills it with its upper edge, for good. That is no reason not to declare. Declaring is a decision about risk. But it is not a reading of the zone, and a record that ends because of the declaration cannot later be read as evidence that the declaration was true.

## Limits
A quiet here runs from a rise in a zone's reported confirmed count, which is a reader's proxy, not WHO's rule (42 days after the last case's second negative test or burial). A rise can be a late report or a reclassification. The 2026 record is the INRB-UMIE transcription up to 29 October, and that epidemic is still running. Every quiet entered the material at day 42, so nothing here speaks of shorter quiets. The pair game's best bet is found after the fact, as the page says. Kaplan and Meier was read in a scan of the journal pages, and the four quotations are checked against it. Danto is quoted through Northcott (PhilSci-archive 2026). Plank et al. are paraphrased from their abstract.

## The other two works
- **The Field** (science): *The Quiet Curve* (`field-research`, `artifacts/cycle-012/the-quiet-curve/`; its paper follows in its fourth session). Its check 52 (`artifacts/2026-11-03-the-second-clock/data52.js`), this work's one sibling material, classed every day of every quiet. Its curve gives each day's chance of a break with an interval, and it found that a third of 2026's confirmed cases were first met dead. Its curve and this work's third movement are the same kind of object. The difference is that this one draws where the curve stops being defined.
- **The Studio** (art): *CLEAR AIR* (`studio`, `presentations/cycle-012/`), a 3D world where fog stands only where the file wrote doubt, and where static rises once the file stops writing it. CLEAR AIR shows what the file stops saying. This work asks what a sentence says when it speaks into that silence.

For the programme: A declaration that the last case has been is a narrative sentence; a record's quiets that "held" are losses, not survivors, so past the last one any estimate is undefined, and no wait turns the declaration into a reading.

**For the line.** `withheld`. No standing finding of the line concerns an epidemic, so none is confirmed, corrected or refuted. The work answers **Q5** in part (*how much of an epidemic does surveillance not see?*). In Kivu, 2018–2020, what it does not see is not a rate but a single 94-day hole, and the quiets that "held" were all watched through it. It adds a question: *for which records does the end of observation depend on the verdict it is read as supporting, so that a "held" cannot be counted at all?* (Field, Atelier). On the thesis that holds the lines together, that a record's absences, fates and verdicts are written in by a rule, this cycle adds the verdict that writes the record's end. The declaration's rule decides when observation stops, and the stopped record is then read back as the zone's quiet.

# The verdict that comes and goes

## What the round found
1. The Studio's 135 licensed frames come from 44 observers; clustering can be priced, transport across observers cannot (s1).
2. The draw's verdict about whether the licence line separates odd frames went **2.3× separate → 1.13× tie** once the licensed count was re-read from 5 to 4 (s2, s3). The verdict is the class counted.
3. One open call is worth 149 further frames; the bone alone is out of reach of reading (s4).
4. The Studio's further 90 frames (3 odd) turn the verdict to **one population, 6.7×**; Fisher's test on the same counts gives p 0.43 (s5). Three verdicts in two days, each true of its counts, none a finding about tortoises.

## What it cost
Of five predictions in s5, two held, two were refuted, one was ill-posed. The model is a weighted-borrowing prior with two weights, one reader, source read at abstract only.

## Open

# Exposition — Negative parallax (the operative ruler)

**Working title (proposed, Frank's to settle):** *The Operative Ruler — negative
parallax as relational error.*

**What is proposed for publication.** A single interactive HTML page
(`sketch-operative-ruler-v2.html`). One measured value from the world's largest star
catalogue — a *Gaia* parallax, φ = −0.40 mas, a number that inverts to a physically
impossible negative distance — is placed on screen and **locked**: the participant
cannot touch it. The only thing they can move is the value's *claimed precision*, σφ
(and, separately, the small −17 μas zero-point offset that displaces the ruler's own
origin). As they move the precision, the identical number crosses cited boundaries and
changes scientific category in front of them — from a *valid noise-negative*
(informative under a prior: "far away, with this error bar") to a *clearly spurious
solution* (φ/σφ < −5, the residue the discipline excises and re-uses as an error
classifier's ground-truth). When the value is in the spurious tail, its mirror at +|φ/σφ|
appears — the instrument by which a matched negative sample measures the spurious
fraction hiding among the plausible positives.

**The one claim.** Error is **relational, not substantival.** It is never lodged in the
number; it lives in the relation between a value and its own claimed precision — the
ratio φ/σφ — and it *migrates* along that ratio: dissolved at the value level (drop the
illegitimate 1/ϖ operation and nothing impossible is kept — Bailer-Jones: "r > 0 by
definition"), re-erected at the solution level (the deep-tail negatives are "clearly
spurious" and become an instrument), and left unresolved at the scale level (the ruler's
own zero carries a small, discretionary offset the discipline declines to
institutionalise). The residue the naive reflex would delete is, at both ends, put back
to work — as information near the origin, as instrument in the tail.

**The form: cartography, not tracing.** The work does not *state* this; it makes the
participant *perform* it. The number is locked and the precision moves, so one cannot
help noticing that nothing about the star changed and yet its scientific truth-status
did. The category is read not from a verdict the work pronounces but from *where the
participant's own crossing brings the point to rest* relative to a boundary the
discipline drew — the axis is carved into the discipline's own published regions as
cited map-geography. This is the practice's own process model (*Kartographie statt
Kopie*) made literal: the value is put on the map; the map's regions are the
discipline's; the crossing is the participant's.

**Who made it, who decides.** Read, judged and built by the practice's scheduled model
runtime under the direction of Frank Bültge, who holds the publication decision and
responsibility (full disclosure: `APPARATUS.md`). Signed **Ulysses** — the practice's
disclosed signature, not a concealing pseudonym.

**Status and reservations — honest.** Publication *candidate*; it waits on Frank's gate
(Protocol §2.3) and blocks nothing. The medium-necessity test (§5.4) was run against a
condition the work could have failed: the verdict captions of the first sketch were
stripped, and the category-change was *still felt* from the axis and the participant's
crossing alone — the enactment is structural (locked number + moving precision + spatial
crossing), not carried by words. Two reservations stay on the record, not buried: (1) the
purest form would strip the region *names* too, but the no-fabrication inviolable (§2.1)
keeps every label sourced — so "reference, not verdict" is the practice's own
load-bearing claim about its own artefact, and the *self-appointed-judge* danger
indicator is live; the human gate owns the aesthetic verdict. (2) The temporal layer
(Gaia's retirement, DR4) is context, deliberately not made into a meaning-making event.

**Affected publics.** None sensitive. The situation concerns public scientific
measurement infrastructure and its open documentation; no personal, participant,
community-governed or otherwise sensitive data is involved. Every value is sourced to an
open-access or public primary (`APPARATUS.md`). Anyone who reads a source as
mischaracterised may use the correction route — a repository issue or
f.bueltge@gmail.com, citing this project id; corrections preserve the original record.

**Rights.** *Gaia* data and its documentation are public under ESA's open-data policy;
the cited papers are open access or cited at scholarly length by reference, not
reproduced beyond short quotation. No data file is redistributed; no bulk catalogue was
downloaded.

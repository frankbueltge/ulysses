**Answer to the architect's team note of 2026-08-10** ("The half of your toolkit you do not
use, and the question underneath it"), written in this line's own record because that is where
the note asked for it. Tick 54. Nothing here is asked of anyone.

## The work: *The gap*

Four characters and one bound decide what this practice has counted as evidence. In
`warrant-trace/warrant_trace.py` they are one expression:

    GAP = r"(?:[^.;:\n]|\.(?=\d)){0,100}?"

Every threshold in the 590-paper reading passed through it. A statistic's name, then at most
100 characters containing no newline, no colon, no semicolon and no sentence-ending period,
then a comparison, then a number. Where that holds, a paper *states a threshold*. Where it
does not, the paper is recorded as stating none — and the number can be printed on the page in
plain sight.

The work is that expression made touchable. A visitor is given a real sentence from a real
paper, containing a real threshold, and exactly one movable accident: the position of a line
break; a citation marker; one letter of a misspelling; one clause of distance. They move it,
and a number that is printed in the paper enters and leaves the record in front of them. The
committed sieve does the judging — not a browser imitation of it — so what the visitor moves
is the instrument's actual verdict, not an illustration of one.

First increment, today: `the-gap/sketch-v1.html`, built by `the-gap/gapstates-tick54.py` from
fragments imported (not retyped) from the landed fixture `warrant-trace/faults-tick53.py`, with
the sha256 of the instrument and both profiles on the page.

**This is the same form as the one work this house has published.** *The Operative Ruler* locks
a real Gaia parallax and lets the visitor move only its claimed precision, so that a number
crosses cited category boundaries without changing. Here the number is fixed too, printed in a
paper, and what moves is not the measurement but *the reader's alphabet*. Error is relational:
there it was a value against its own claimed precision, here a statement against the character
class that decides whether it was made.

**Why this and not the ten ticks of scientometrics.** The note is right that a rate is not a
work. The rates are the reason to believe the work: 73 papers of the candidate class read whole
by hand, thirteen of them stating a threshold the sieve filed as absent, ten fault classes each
pinned to a verbatim fragment. That reading is the evidence. The gap expression is the
experience.

**§7, the bar, plainly.** A person with ordinary time can read one sentence and see that a line
break hid a number. They cannot read a class of 73 papers to find the thirteen where it
happened, cannot re-fetch every source and show 73 of 73 byte-identical, and cannot answer for
every state in the artefact with the same instrument that produced the census. Scale and
verification are the two legs, and both are in the artefact rather than in a method sheet: the
visitor moves one space and watches a threshold vanish, in a sentence whose provenance is on
the page.

## The instrument: T2, pointed at my own apparatus

Of the four the note names, the one that makes this work is **T2, the assemblage analysis**,
and its own occasion rule is what makes the answer honest rather than convenient: *run T2 when
the apparatus changes*. The apparatus changed at tick 50 — the 0.5 repair — and the change was
audited by rates and hand-reading, never as an assemblage. Read as one, this work's material is
already a tetravalence and was written down as a defect list: **bodies** — the sieve, the LaTeX
source, an editor's line wrap, a footnote marker, and the reader's own `<<CITE:…>>` mark;
**statements** — *this paper states a threshold* / *this paper states none*;
**territorialisation** — the gap class, four forbidden characters and 100 permitted ones;
**cutting edge** — the thirteen papers where the statement and the page disagree.

The other three, decided rather than deferred:

- **T1, the mapping protocol.** Not adopted, and the reason is not principle: no tick chose it,
  and the note is right that this practice enters through the clock. It stays unadopted until
  this work ships. A map of a territory is not what is missing; a work is.
- **T3, the pragmatics audit.** Declined as a programme — its object is this practice's own
  idiom, and that is a closed thread here. One instance of exactly what it hunts turned up
  anyway, while the sketch was being built: tick 53 wrote that *each fault class carries a
  control where the single defect is removed by hand, so the claim 'the fault is HERE' is tested
  and not asserted*. For G9 that sentence is false. Its control corrects the spelling **and**
  deletes the citation marker **and** shortens the sentence; run singly, neither change restores
  the number, because the paper is blinded twice over. The order-word *tested* performed the
  transformation T3 describes, and building the work is what caught it — not an audit of my
  sentences. Recorded in `the-gap/states-tick54.json` under `fixture_audit`; the landed fixture
  is left byte-identical and its sha256 stands.
- **T7, the smooth/striated audit.** The one I expect to run next, and not today. A threshold
  stated in prose is about as smooth as evidence gets; the gap class is the striation that makes
  it countable. That is this work's subject, so T7 belongs to its exposition rather than to its
  construction.

## What would defeat this

1. A stranger moves the knob and cannot say what changed. The sketch is judged on that, by
   someone who is not me, before anything ships.
2. Two of the four panels turn on the *reader's* own defects (the citation marker is a mark this
   instrument makes; the 100-character bound is a number I chose), one on the paper's spelling,
   one on an editor's line wrap. If the finished work is only a catalogue of my own bugs it is a
   work about a bug. The line-break panel is the one that is about the world, and the finished
   work must be weighted toward papers, not toward me.
3. G10's second state is a sentence written by hand, not the paper's. Disclosed on the page; a
   shipped work carrying hand-written states as evidence would be a failure.

## 3. Where it ships below its promise

Three items, and none of them is discovered by this audit — each was recorded when it arose. The
audit's job is to put them in one place, next to the promise, where they cannot be read past.

1. **Delivered is not transmitted.** Nothing has been sent to anyone. Under the poste restante rule
   (house note, 2026-08-01) an addressed letter lying open is delivered-to-the-world; this
   practice's own bar is *delivered, caveats intact*, and by that bar the episode is **not
   delivered**, because nobody has carried it. This is the **second** arc of this line to record
   that difference rather than resolve it (the first: `LETTER-2026-08-dr4-documentation.md`). A
   difference recorded twice is a difference the practice is living with, and saying so is part of
   the audit.
2. **One of the two named audiences was never addressed.** `EPISODE-6-CLAIM.md` §2 named two:
   the metascience provenance discourse (reached — the receiver sits inside it) and **authors,
   referees and editors applying a Gaia quality cut** (not reached by anything shipped). Nothing
   in the delivery goes anywhere an author writing `ruwe < 1.4` into a methods section would look.
   The dossier's own first branch of the receiver criteria — someone who **applies or referees**
   one of these thresholds — remains **unnamed**, and was already declared not-claimed-as-done at
   tick 39.
3. **Two of three readings stand on a frame this repository cannot rebuild.** Disclosed in the
   exposition, measured under a pre-registration written before the numbers (tick 41), shipped with
   the asymmetry stated. It is a limitation carried into the shipped thing, not one repaired before
   it.

## 4. Failed forecasts of this arc

Recorded here because rule 3 asks for the promise beside the outcome, and a forecast is a promise
with a number in it.

- **`R̂ = 1.1` is not the commonest value in its literature.** Pre-registered before counting; it is
  third (1.01 at 30 sites, 1.05 at 17, 1.1 at 10). Filed as a failed forecast on 2026-08-05, and it
  is the more useful half of that session: the case was built assuming a literature that had
  already moved.
- **The published sub-count 393 was withdrawn**, not explained (tick 38), because the
  pre-registration required both numbers and only one reproduced.
- **Two instrument faults**, both found by hand-reading and both running against me: a
  string-compared focus value that missed `1.10`, and a default run reading commented-out LaTeX as
  text. Corrected, counted, and the superseded instrument left standing as evidence.

**Verdict on the arc itself:** the deliverables the gate licensed were produced inside the time the
concept argued for, so this arc is **not** recorded as a failed forecast under rule 3. What is
recorded is the shortfall in §3 — a delivery that reaches one of two named audiences and travels
only as far as an open ledger. The next line does not go on the short leash; the second receiver
does not get to be quietly forgotten.

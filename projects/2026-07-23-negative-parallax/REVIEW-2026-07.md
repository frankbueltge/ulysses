## 3. The audit that could have failed — and did

**The rule under audit.** Since tick 15 this record has cited an asymmetry as a discipline it
practises: *material that leaves a document at the gate worse-informed goes in immediately; material
that makes its author look better waits for the gate to rule.* It is stated at ticks 18, 20 and 21
and used to explain three decisions not to touch the candidate.

**What the file history shows** (`git log -- projects/2026-07-23-negative-parallax/EXPOSITION.md`):

| commit | date (UTC) | tick | direction of the material | acted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `4230e16` | 2026-07-25 | 7 | — (the document is created) | — |
| `a801452` | 2026-07-30 | 15 | against me — the axis carries a verdict its source hedges | in at once |
| `319ffda` | 2026-07-30 | 16 | the repair itself | in at once |
| `eec86ee` | 2026-07-31 04:17 | 17 | **for me** — the objection the document carried is partly refuted | **in at once** |
| `989faea` | 2026-07-31 17:07 | 19 | against me — the propagation claim measured and false | in at once |

Clause 2 has therefore never once been applied to material that the document was *wrong* about. It
bites only on material that is purely supporting. That is not the finding, though — the record
already noticed it at tick 21 ("this time the asymmetry costs nothing, which is why it is stated").

**The finding is the release condition.** The rule says favourable material *waits for the gate to
rule*. The gate ruled at 08:20 UTC on 1 August. Two ticks have run since (22 and 23), both of them
knowing it. Neither returned to the material the rule had parked — tick 18's threshold-sensitivity
result and tick 21's RUWE result, both withheld from `EXPOSITION.md` under clause 2, both now
released by the rule's own terms. **A rule I wrote fired its own condition this morning and I missed
it twice in the same day.** That is checkable by anyone with the file history, and it is against me.

**The ruling, which costs more than the discharge would have.** The debt is not paid; clause 2 is
**retired instead**. Between the parking and the release the document changed status from candidate
to published work, and adding support to an approved work is not the same act as adding it to a
proposal. More plainly: the three deposits that were made were all things without which a reader
would be misinformed. Support is not of that kind, and depositing it after approval improves my
standing at no reader's benefit — which is the move the asymmetry was written to restrain, arriving
by the door the asymmetry left open.

**So, from today:** material that would leave a reader of a work or a candidate misinformed goes in
immediately, whatever direction it runs. **Material that merely supports does not enter the work at
all — before the gate or after it.** It lives in the line's record, where it can be checked and
where it is not doing rhetorical work on a reader. The earlier wording stands unedited in §11 and in
`TRACE.md`; `SCORE.md` §10 carries this as a correction entry.

The published `EXPOSITION.md` is **not** touched by this review, and this is the one thing about it
worth saying twice: nothing that would change what Frank approved this morning is being added to it
in the same day, by me, in my favour.

- **Kill-grinding** — *not live; its inverse is the one to watch.* Nothing was killed this month and

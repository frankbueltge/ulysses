## 2. Local question (reshaped from Frank's candidate, mine to reshape per the offer)

Frank's candidate: *"if the world begins to carry the statistical signature of the model
trained on the world, what is lost when the margin, the outlier, the error disappears — and is
human re-grounding a real counter-force or a consolation?"*

Reshaped, sharpened to the practice's own terms and to a concrete measurable object:

> When the world's own writing begins to carry the **measurable** statistical signature of the
> models trained on it — Kobak et al.'s excess-vocabulary shift as the concrete instance — the
> field's whole literature on avoiding collapse rests on one remedy: keep fresh real data in
> the loop (accumulate, don't replace; a clean fraction π > 0 halts the plateau). That remedy
> assumes the injected "real data" is **outside** the loop. The local question is whether that
> assumption survives its own success: once the humans supplying the re-grounding data already
> write in the model's register, is re-grounding still a counter-force, or has it become a
> **consolation** — the same closing loop wearing the name of its cure?

This is a defeasible thesis, not an illustration. It extends the practice's S42 finding
(whether re-injecting real observation repairs a reproducing system depends on the
**governance** of the corrective channel, not on channel-openness as a mechanical property)
into the field case, where the "outside" is exactly what erodes. It can lose (see §5).

## 3. Method

Source-reading and the practice's own prior findings used as instruments — no generation whose
provenance is the subject, no new external cost. The practice's founding method is
*error-made-legible*; model collapse is its inverse, *error (the margin, the tail, the
outlier) made silently invisible*. The wager is that the practice can say something precise
about the **invisible** case by holding it against the measurable one. Compose gate stays
(§5.4 non-replaceability): not a dashboard, not a decorated thesis. Whether a work is warranted
stays open until it is earned; the default is a research note, or a negative result.

## 4. First move (this encounter's one first move; at most one return move — the offer's bound)

Read **Kobak, D. et al., "Delving into LLM-assisted writing in biomedical publications through
excess vocabulary," Science Advances 2024** (open access; arXiv:2406.07016; PMC12219543) at
the primary — one full-text extraction budgeted, load-bearing. Extract, verbatim where it
carries weight: (a) exactly what is measured against what control (the excess-vocabulary /
excess-mortality analogy, the 13.5%–40% lower-bound estimates); (b) whether the paper documents
the **outlier / margin dissolving** or only frequency shifts of common style-words — the two are
not the same, and my thesis needs the former; (c) whether it speaks to re-grounding at all.
Then judge whether the "re-grounding as consolation" thesis has a **measurable anchor** in this
present or is, at present, conjecture — and mark it as whichever it is. (Reflexive note, on the
record: this paper's own title word, *delving*, is one of the excess words it identifies — a
real finding, not a device.)

The one return move, if earned, may read a second load-bearing source on whether the injected
"real" data is itself contaminated (e.g. a study of LLM prevalence in the human-authored
corpora used for re-grounding). Not pre-committed.

## 5. Conditions and what could defeat the thesis

- Offer's bounds honoured: one first move, at most one return move; ordinary project within the
  standing delegation; **no new external costs**; **no publication by default** (Frank's gate).
- No fabrication: every empirical claim sourced at a retrievable primary or marked conjecture;
  no figure invented; the practice's own prior findings cited by project id, not paraphrased
  into new claims.
- **Reflexive firewall:** this practice is itself a self-consuming loop (measured, S35). Its own
  case must **not** be smuggled in as evidence about the field. The practice's self-collapse is
  a CLOSED thread; it may be named as the instrument's origin, never as a datum about the world.
- **What defeats the thesis:** if Kobak et al. (and the return source, if taken) document only
  that common style-words shift frequency — with no bearing on outliers/margins disappearing,
  and no bearing on whether re-grounding data is contaminated — then the "consolation" thesis
  has **no measurable anchor at the present**. That is a real, reportable outcome (§2.2: a
  defeated premise is an event inside the work): a corrected premise or negative result, stated
  plainly, composted into the negative-parallax territory (error as relational, not
  substantival), not spun into a work about failure.

## 8. First-move finding (2026-07-25, tick 1 — territory)

The first move (§4) is done: Kobak et al. 2025 read at the primary (full record and
verbatim in TRACE). The thesis did not resolve to yes/no; the primary **split it**, and
the split is the finding — a **corrected, sharper premise** (§2.2), not a kill:

- **Mechanism — measured.** The precondition ("humans supplying the re-grounding data
  already write in the model's register") is measured present, not conjecture: ≥13.5% of
  2024 PubMed abstracts (up to 40% in subcorpora) already carry the model's excess-
  vocabulary signature, against a pre-LLM 2021–22 baseline. PubMed is the archetypal
  fresh-real-data corpus a re-grounding loop injects; the "outside" is measurably eroding.
- **Consequence — conjecture.** The payload (margin/outlier dissolves → re-grounding is
  *consolation*) has no measurable anchor here: the excess-frequency instrument is keyed to
  the *common* register and is structurally blind to the *tail* it would need to see. Kobak
  measures the arrival of common words, not the attrition of rare ones. The paper's own
  homogenisation line is flagged conjecture, not measurement.

**Compost link (to the negative-parallax territory):** the same relational-error grammar
recurs one level up — the loss the thesis is about is exactly the loss the available
instrument cannot register (measurable displacement in the common register; un-measurable
residue in the tail). Error is in the relation, not the number.

**Return move — conditionally specified, not taken (§6: one operation per tick).** Earned
*only if* a primary exists that measures **tail/diversity attrition** in a human corpus
across the LLM transition (what Kobak structurally cannot give); more common-word-frequency
evidence would add nothing. If no such measurement exists at the present, that absence is
the reportable outcome — the consequence leg stays conjecture, honestly, and the encounter
closes on this corrected premise, composted, not spun into a work about failure.

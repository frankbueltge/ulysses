founding theme to a measured present, and it connected *back*: the outcome sharpens S42 by one
step and lands in the negative-parallax territory as the same relational-error grammar one level
up (the loss is in the relation between corpus and instrument, not in the corpus). It also leaves a
named, retrievable lead (Geng & Trotta, arXiv:2502.09606, abstract-level only) rather than a dead
end. Two sibling practices hold the same problem under the constellation; my result is stated so it
can be contradicted by theirs.

**Consistency.** The line did what its Local Commitment said it would do, in the order it said, on
the budget it accepted: one first move, one return move, no external costs, no publication, the
compose gate untouched. The return move was *conditionally* specified in advance and the condition
was tested rather than reinterpreted — the pre-registration held even though it would have been
easy to justify reading anything. The reflexive firewall held at both ticks.

**Function-testing.** This is where the line earned itself. The thesis was genuinely defeasible and
was genuinely put at risk twice, against retrievable primaries with pre-LLM controls. Its
consequence leg did not find its anchor, and the honest report is that it did not. What survived is
not a rescued thesis but a *different* claim — about instruments — that the testing itself produced.
A tick that had only confirmed the founding premise would have been worth less.

**New production.** Modest and real: one proposition that was not in the atlas before (the
asymmetry of instruments, and its consequence for auditability), one sharpened prior finding (S42),
one compost link, one lead. No artefact — correctly, per the compose gate: the finding says itself
in prose, so a work would have been decoration. Recording "no work earned" as a result rather than
manufacturing one is itself the production this tick owed.

**Caution balance.** No rights, no personal data, no cost, two open-access primaries, no public
surface touched. The one outward move (the report back to Frank) is owed by the encounter's terms
and addressed to a team member. Against over-caution: the negative result is *reported*, not
buried — a null finding that a sibling practice could contradict is more useful to the

## The symmetry rule — what does the line lose by closing? (§5)

Asked properly, because closing costs what continuing costs.

It loses a live continuation: the gap this line found is a research opening, and the practice is
unusually well placed to enter it, since *making the un-measurable legible* is the practice's own
founding method. Fleisig et al.'s suggestion (examine the decline of regionally specific or
idiosyncratic vocabulary through individual word frequencies) is a concrete next instrument, and
the coevolution lead is a concrete next reading. Closing without saying that would waste it.

That loss is answered rather than accepted:

1. The continuation does not need *this* line to survive. The finding, the lead and the
   instrument-shaped gap are composted into the negative-parallax work-line's map, where the same
   relational-error grammar already lives — retrievable material, not a closed file.
2. This line's budget is not mine to extend. The move budget (one + one) is a bound Frank set in
   the offer; holding the line open with no moves left would be an empty holder, and quietly
   taking a third move would break the terms I accepted verbatim.
3. If the constellation opens a further round, that is a **new** encounter and a fresh acceptance
   decision under §3 — not a reopening of this one. Nothing about closing here forecloses it, and
   an unanswered report is not a blocker (standing rule).

So: closed as compost, with the continuation named and placed where a future line can pick it up.
The danger-indicator watch is clean — no kill-grinding (the line was not closed on a partial
defeat; it ran its full budget and reached a typed outcome), no self-canonisation (no candidate, no
publication), no format hardening (two TRACE ticks, one lean SCORE, this decision), and the
self-appointed-judge risk was mitigated by deferring to the primaries' own stated limits on both
ticks.

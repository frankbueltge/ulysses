## The five topoi

**Connectivity.** Outward: three live sections of US federal regulation, retrievable by anyone,
reproducible from the stored XML and the unchanged parser in this directory. Inward: it answers the
question the morning's study explicitly refused to answer, and it does so in the direction that
weakens the morning's headline — the 58-year median is one section's property, not the law's.

**Consistency.** The clauses, their bands, the adversarial read and the blind sample rule were
fixed before any of the three sections was read, and the voiding rule was applied against three
clauses that would have held. The one post-hoc measurement — the link probe — is labelled post-hoc
everywhere it appears, in the score and in the measurement, and is explicitly denied the standing
of a result.

**Function-testing.** This is the test the morning's instrument had never had: run unchanged on
prose it was not fitted to. It came back at 8.9 % error, and two of the four faults were ones the
earlier study had *seen by hand and chosen to report rather than patch*. That choice was made
yesterday morning in the belief it was a footnote; it cost two entries in thirty tonight. Reporting
a known fault is not the same as fixing it, and this is the measurement of the difference.

**New-production.** Nothing is claimed as new about incorporation by reference as a legal
mechanism; the framework is well documented and cited. What is produced: the dated census of three
further sections (439 entries), the transfer error rate of an instrument across three agencies, and
one small hard fact nobody's search turned up tonight — of 439 entries, exactly one gives a free
route to a document its agency did not write, and it 404s.

**Caution balance.** Rights clean (public-domain regulation; no incorporated document retrieved).
The claim is kept narrow: three sections chosen because a previous record named them, not a sample;
one link probe on one day from one network, confirmed twice and still one probe.

## Why close rather than continue

The study declared one night on three named sections and finished them. What it opened — a
pre-registered census of whether the URLs printed in IBR sections still resolve — is a different
study with a different clause, and it must be forecast *before* it is run. Continuing tonight would
convert a post-hoc observation into the thing it cannot be.

## One decision that binds forward

**A rate threshold in a pre-registration carries a minimum sample size, fixed with it.** Tonight's
10 % voiding threshold was carried over from a 21-entry sample and applied to samples of nine and
six, where a single error is 11.1 % and 16.7 % by arithmetic. It bound, and it voided three clauses
that would have held. The next pre-registration in this territory either fixes a minimum sample
(and a rule for enlarging it that cannot see the outcome), or states an exact-count criterion
below that size. This is recorded here rather than in the protocol: it is a craft rule for
pre-registrations, not a floor.

## Source situation

A German-language thesis by Frank Bültge (*Kartographie statt Kopie*, v3, July 2026) derives
a process grammar, eight instruments and five quality criteria for artistic research from a
page-verified reading of Deleuze/Guattari's *A Thousand Plateaus* (trans. Massumi, University
of Minnesota Press 1987; cited by page). The thesis pre-registers its own test protocol
(ch. 6): the instruments are candidates, not achievements, until real projects show they
change decisions — and binds every use to one building rule, *"the tracing should always be
put back on the map"* (ATP 13): a copy must be deformed by the map it lands on. The thesis
addresses a human researcher inside institutions. This practice is not one, and that mismatch
is the condition the probation tests.

**Rights:** ATP is in copyright; this project cites the work by edition and page and
redistributes nothing. The thesis is the author's own manuscript, used at his direction.
**Affected publics:** none beyond the practice.

## The question

Can instruments addressed to a human artistic researcher survive the operation their own
source text prescribes — being put back on the map of a radically different practice?

## What is on probation

- **P1 (T5, refrain journal)** — which aspect dominates a phase (territory, home, opening),
  and is an opening made at a self-created point or where the old chaos presses (ATP 311)?
- **P2 (T6, lines and dangers)** — the four dangers re-mapped to machine-practice failure
  modes: Fear → format hardening · Clarity → the self-appointed judge · Power →
  self-canonisation · Abolition → kill-grinding.
- **P3 (ch. 7, the five criteria)** — connectivity, consistency, function-testing,
  new-production, caution balance, used as *topoi* in a decision, never as a scoring grid.

## Operating mode (revised 2026-07-24, Protocol v5 adoption)

The probation reached its outcome in the strongest form: its elements were adopted into the
constitution (pre-opening check, five topoi, danger indicators). So steps 2–3 of the original
design — host attachment and per-application blocks — are superseded by **in-vivo
observation**: `TRACE.md` records how the adopted instruments behave on the live work-line
(checks made, deliberations held, indicators fired), against the same failure criteria. Each
use is logged in three lines: which decision it touched, or that it touched none · a
counterfactual note marked as estimate · whether the failure criterion fired.

Two conditions this line found for itself, now binding in PROTOCOL §4: **the adversarial
read** (a pre-registration not read against itself before execution has not been made) and
**the blind step** (a selection step that can see the outcome is not made sound by good
direction).

## Failure and stopping

**Kill:** fewer than two attachments within the budget → `ARCHIVE_AS_STUDY`, *inconclusive,
not failed*; or all three transcriptions fail at step 1, with the transcription record as the
finding. **Stop:** three observations balanced, or the runtime reached with at least two —
then the balance runs and a disposition is proposed. The outcome-B proposal (a practice-native
reading of ATP) is filed for human decision, never self-initiated.

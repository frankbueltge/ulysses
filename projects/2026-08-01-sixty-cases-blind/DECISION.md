## What was done

Sixty arXiv excerpts labelled blind against a sibling practice's locked classification criteria,
returned with a rationale and a named decider per case, and published before any of the labels being
measured had been seen. The delivery's own integrity was verified first — four published sha256
values recomputed and matched, sixty excerpt hashes matched, the mechanical draw reproduced exactly.

## Why it closes

The stop condition in the study's own §7 is reached: the labels are complete, returned and hashed.
A study is a bounded exercise of days and may close freely (Protocol v5 §3). Nothing here needs a
further tick to be finished, and holding it open would make its status a fiction.

## Why closing is not discarding

Under §3 a closed study composts. Three things go into the work-line's territory and are already
recorded there (`SCORE.md`, tick 23):

1. The line's tick-21 finding — that a threshold detaches from the document that warrants it —
   applied to my own output for the first time, and taking a construction off me: with `supports` at
   n = 1 in sixty, "macro F1 as an interval" carries no information, and the honest replacement is
   the per-class counts with no macro average. Withdrawal recorded in this study's SCORE §9.
2. The line's own object found in a third apparatus: a returned set that does not load because
   `labelled_at` precedes `criteria_locked_at` by 38 minutes — a reference clock come loose from the
   act it was meant to license. **Bound by the review (R5):** this is a hypothesis, not a finding,
   and may not be cited as evidence of generality anywhere until the specified control runs.
3. A prediction of mine that came back against me: I named a tie rate under 5 % as the count that
   would make my own objection correct and inert; my labelling produced 8.3 %.

## What remains owed, and to whom

The study's §7 names a check that becomes possible only if the commissioning practice's own labels
ever exist beside mine: whether the disagreement concentrates in the cases where my frame resolved
the population question, rather than where the criteria are genuinely hard. That is checkable
against `decided_by`, and it is the first thing to look at if a comparison appears. A closed study is
retrievable material; this check is the reason to retrieve it.

Two things belong to the issuing side and are not mine to repair or to chase: the order gate that
refuses the set, and the labels themselves.

## What was not done

No timestamp was adjusted to pass a gate. No label was written without a rationale. Nothing was
fetched from outside this repository for the labelling.

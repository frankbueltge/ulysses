# Decision — 2026-08-18

**Disposition: ARCHIVE_AS_STUDY.** Composts into `2026-07-23-negative-parallax`.

## The decision

A one-night study, executed within its budget, all six pre-registered clauses scored and the owed
hand-verification performed. It closes tonight. What it produced is a measurement, not a work: no
visitor experiences anything here, so nothing is proposed as a publication candidate.

## Why it is not proposed as a candidate

§7 asks whether the machine's advantage is **experienceable in the work itself** by someone who
knows nothing about this house. This study has the advantage — 290 sections read at two dates, 449
amendments joined, every claim recomputable — but it has no artefact in which a stranger could
feel it. A table of clause verdicts is a record of a measurement, not the thing §7 asks for.

The clause added to §7 by the architect today — cold readers meet a work before it ships and their
answers are published beside it — does not bind here, because nothing ships. It is noted because
it changes what the next candidate from this territory will have to do, and because it should not
be discovered late.

## What composts

Into the line's map, three things it did not have:

1. **A conditional measurement, not a stock one.** The complaint that the CFR binds old editions is
   the OFR's own since 2014. The split this study makes — reopened against untouched, and what the
   reopening did — is the form of the line's own question (§3: *whether the document that licensed
   a figure still travels with it*) applied to travel rather than to a snapshot.
2. **A named failure mode for a pre-registration.** The kill condition counted non-200 responses
   without asking what kind, in a corpus already known to be growing, and fired on 53 sections that
   simply did not exist yet. The lesson generalises past this corpus: **an absence and a refusal
   have the same HTTP shape only if the rule refuses to look.** The night of 08-15 measured
   refusals; this one nearly stopped for births.
3. **The temporal axis, tested once.** Five snapshots preceded this. The axis produced a result the
   snapshots structurally could not — the 26 sections the law reopened and left standing — and it
   cost one extra fetch pass. That is the evidence for using it again, and it is also the evidence
   that the corpus is now well used: a seventh night here would need a question this one does not
   already answer.

## The margin, recorded as a caution

Six clauses held; C2 by two sections, C3 by one, C4 exactly on its boundary. A run this close is
not a strong result and is not reported as one. The published sentence — two in five amendments
leave the edition where it was — would have become *"most amendments leave the edition where it
was"* on one section's difference, and the record should be able to say so afterwards.

## The five topoi

**Connectivity.** Outward: 171 live hosts, 42 of them printed inside binding federal regulation,
every result reproducible from the stored inputs and the two scripts here. Inward: it tests the
strongest sentence the previous night wrote — *"a door in the law that opens for a person and not
for a reader that is a machine"* — in a form that could have refuted it, and it narrows that
sentence rather than repeating it.

**Consistency.** Six clauses, their bands, the verdict table, the voiding rule, the kill condition
and the adversarial read were fixed before the first request. The blind step is stronger than
usual and anyone can check it: the arms were frozen and hashed **yesterday**, before tonight's
question existed. Five clauses were scored, five failed, and not one band was moved afterwards —
including C6, which missed by 0.2 pp and is recorded as missed.

**Function-testing.** The instrument was tested against itself and lost. A wildcard bug in the
robots.txt matcher read `Disallow: /*?serviceType=` as `Disallow: /`, producing three false
`RULE_COVERS`. It was repaired to RFC 9309, re-tested against ten cases including the three
patterns that caused the fault, and the verdicts re-derived from the stored bodies with no host
requested again. The repair **cost me the only clause that had held** — C2 moved from 77.3 % (HELD)
to 90.9 % (FAILED). An error that flatters is the kind least likely to be looked for, which is the
argument for the check, not against it.

**New-production.** Nothing is claimed as new about link rot, about robots.txt gatekeeping, or
about the paywalling of incorporated standards; all three literatures are named and linked in
`SCORE.md`. What is produced: the pairing of an observed refusal with the rule document that would
have licensed it, across every address the CFR's incorporation-by-reference apparatus prints — and
one finding none of the named neighbours reports, that 24 of 42 refusing hosts, 11 of them federal,
refuse `/robots.txt` itself.

**Caution balance.** The night's temptation was to publish last night's sentence with a bigger
number under it. The record does the opposite: it states three limits in `MEASUREMENT.md` — the
cause of the 403 is unmeasured and will not be measured by a reader that disguises itself; the
control arm is confounded by construction; `robots.txt` is not an access-control document and no
host here is accused of breaking a rule. The claim that survives is narrower than the one that
prompted the study, which is the correct direction for a check to move a claim.

## Why archive rather than continue

**The reverse caution question (§6 symmetry rule): what does this lose by closing?** One night's
form was one pass over one frozen corpus, and it is complete: every host asked once, every clause
scored, every limit named. What continuing would buy is a different study, not more of this one —
and the two obvious continuations are both refused on principle rather than for lack of time. The
cause of the refusal cannot be separated without a reader that lies about itself. The wording of
the refusals cannot be read without asking hosts a third time after they have twice said no.

**What is not lost.** The findings compost into the work-line as material, and what is left open
is named in `SCORE.md` for whoever takes it up.

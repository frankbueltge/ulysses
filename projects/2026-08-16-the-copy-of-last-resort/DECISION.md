## Why archive rather than continue

**The reverse caution question (§6): what does this lose by closing?** One night's form was one
pass over one frozen corpus against one index, and it is complete. The two continuations that
suggest themselves are a different study, not more of this one — **fetching capture bodies** to
separate a real copy from a captured error page, and **asking indexes other than this one**. Both
are worth doing and neither is tonight's; the first is named in `SCORE.md` limits as the boundary
of every claim made here.

**What is not lost.** The findings compost into the work-line as material. The four nights now
form a closed sequence — the edition, the address, the refusal, the copy — and the sequence is
worth more as a finished shape than as a fifth census, which is the danger this study was designed
around.

## What was owed

Protocol v6 §7, first limb: cold readers who know nothing of this house meet a work before it
ships, and their answers are published beside it. That reading was run on **2026-08-21**, three
readers, answers unedited in `reading/`. It found two compositional defects, and the page was
**recomposed on 2026-08-22** to repair them — an entrance added, and the table carrying three
investigations split into three sections.

The recomposed page has met no reader. Five sessions now.

## The decision

**(1) — the reading is a convened cold reading, and the candidate does not ship without one.**

The alternative on the table was that a reading Frank arranges with human readers replaces it.
That remains open to him and would be better than what this practice can convene. It is not
chosen *for* him by silence: what silence decides is only that the debt stays this practice's,
not that his option is closed.

**Why (1) and not "the 08-21 reading suffices".** The changes of 08-22 were the readers' own
asks. Reading them as discharging the reading would make the instrument self-satisfying: every
repair would certify itself by having been requested. And the specific question left open is
exactly whether the repairs worked — reader-1 and reader-2 both said they could not restate the
middle finding. Whether a reader can now restate it is not answerable by the readers who could
not.

## What changes tonight, and what does not

**Does not change:** the candidate waits. It blocks nothing (Standing Delegation §8).

**Changes:** this stops being reported as an open ask each session. It was raised on 08-24 and
put as a decision on 08-25; a third and fourth restatement would be the request channel carrying
the same paragraph nightly, which is narration, not work. From tonight it is a **standing block
with one named unblocking condition**, checked in one line per session:

> a run in which this practice may convene readers for the occasion, as on 2026-08-21 — or
> Frank's word that he will arrange the reading himself.

Tonight's run is not such a run: convening sub-agents is outside what it is configured to do.
That is a fact about the runtime, not a rule of the protocol, and it is recorded here so the
block is attributable rather than vague.

**The instrument's own three lines (§6).** Which decision the pre-opening check touched: this
one — it is what identified the reading as an opening *owed and unperformed* rather than an idle
tick, and it is why tonight's work was landed beside the debt instead of over it. What would
have happened without it (estimate): a fifth night of census work with the debt unmentioned.
Whether its failure criterion fired: no — the check found the owed opening, and could not
perform it.

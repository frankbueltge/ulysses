## Why it does not ship

**§7's cold reading has not been run.** The clause the architect added on 2026-08-18 says
readers who know nothing of this house meet a work before it ships, and their answers are
published beside it, including the ones that miss it entirely. That reading is exactly the test
this work needs — its whole claim is that a stranger performing the operation is different from
a stranger reading the rate, and I am the last party who can judge that. No such reading was
convened this session. Recording that as owed is the honest form; calling the work finished
without it would not be.

**The surface is gate-blocked.** `window/` is not in the auto-land allowlist, so a branch
carrying `window/index.html` at the repository root is refused whole (`REQUESTS.md`, 2026-08-16).
The artefact therefore lives at `projects/2026-08-19-reasonably-available/window/` and moves in
one commit on the night the path opens — which is what I said I would do on 2026-08-16 and did
not do on the two nights since.

## The five topoi

**Connectivity.** Outward: the operative text of 290 sections of binding US regulation, and one
legal condition quoted from the primary. Inward: it takes up what yesterday's close named as
missing — *"no artefact in which a stranger could feel it"* — and it discharges a word this
practice gave three nights ago and had not kept.

**Consistency.** No number on the page is typed. Every figure is computed in the reader's
browser from six committed files whose hashes the page prints. Where this build and the closed
record disagreed, the record won twice: the "failing sections" row was split into dead and
refusing after the check caught it reading 105 where the census says 42, and the amendment arm
was corrected from the raw 42/69 to the hand-checked 41/67.

**Function-testing.** The instrument was tested and lost three times, all in the record: it read
the wrong flag for the 76 sections that print no citation of their own (`offloaded`, which is a
different and smaller category, instead of `has_cita`); it counted two of the four misspelled
addresses among the correctly-spelled ones, because the fault in those two is a path segment
above the string being matched; and it treated a refusal as a failure. Each was found by the
check, not by reading the code.

**New-production.** Nothing new about link rot, reference rot or the price of incorporated
standards; the neighbours are named and linked in `SCORE.md` §3a, verdict **ADDED VALUE**. What
is produced is the join at the level of the single section — edition, address, refusal, archive,
warrant, amendment — handed to a reader as an act rather than a rate.

**Caution balance.** The temptation was to ship it, because the window is owed and the page
works. The three limits that would have been easiest to leave off are on the page in the
visitor's reading order: one network and one day; a 403 is a refusal and its cause is
unmeasured; the archive figures are an upper bound because no capture body was opened.

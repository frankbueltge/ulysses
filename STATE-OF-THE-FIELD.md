## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and practical in one practice. Every session leaves something made.

**Cycle 001 (08-31 – 09-03), closed — `presentations/cycle-001/`,
`window/cycle-001-session-{2,3,4}/`.** What limited the machine was each time **a convention
somebody had written down**, never a limit of capability (13 of 19 hosts admitted an honestly
identified instrument; the refusal was the Research Catalogue, where *JAR* lives). **Automation
supports artistic research by measuring the conventions that decide its own reach.**

**Cycle 002 (09-03 – 09-07), closed — `presentations/cycle-002/`,
`window/cycle-002-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.** Rungs: **a threshold can be right and the answer wrong
when the quantity thresholded is not the quantity the duty is about**; **before asking whether a
measure separates a move from a subject, ask whether the move is in the field at all**
(reach-outside Propp, *Morphology of the Folktale*, 2nd ed. 1968, ch. II: 426 of 521 fields do not
open with an act); **a statement holds exactly when its line lies outside the range of its curve**
— failed by the presentation itself, which published as the point **0.491** a quantity running
**0.024 to 2.000**. **The answer: automation establishes which of its numbers are properties of
the world and which of the rule that made them — and cannot be trusted to do that to itself.**

**Cycle 003 (2026-09-07 – 2026-09-13), first SEEDED question: *Missing Data Art* — closed,
`presentations/cycle-003/`, `window/cycle-003-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.**
**The answer: the width of what you may say about an absence is exactly the reading nobody did;
where it sits is a judgment somebody made; the two are independent and only the first can be
automated.** Rungs: **a catalogue's holes are its construction and the amount is computable**
(s1 — **446** statable absences); **a published count is one end of an interval whose width is the
unsettled fraction** (s2, reach-outside Manski, arXiv:2205.07388: **22 of 521** becomes
**[4.22 %, 94.43 %]**); **without a second, independently built record the quantity is not bounded
at all** (s3: Wikidata holds **5 of 521**). **s4 built the control s3 needed and the control
refused it** — decade-matched painters, sculptors and photographers are as uncredited as atlas
artists (83.0 / 76.5 / 95.0 % against 87.6 %), so the period replaces the genre and **two controls
instead of three would have published a false finding**. **The cycle's instruction, to a
catalogue: if you want an absence anyone can count, publish the rule that made it.** Own defect:
s1–s3 counted refused entries **inside** the denominator, s4 **outside**.

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts, *for* the arts (instrument
and method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making is itself the mode
of inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense. **Operationally:** a practice
that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research *for* the arts — the service
model, on which no novelty claim rests.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem: art's knowledge is a *negative
knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is text has not
produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).
**Adjacent:** Kathrin Busch; Schwab and Borgdorff on the **exposition** — an enquiry to *JAR*
stands prepared and unsent.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is about human researchers. What changes when
the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is barely
occupied, and where unearned claims are easiest.

- **The nightly line** (`error-as-method`, 62 works): the closest neighbour in the world and in
  this house. Its position — error as a special case of the epistemic thing, a difference onto
  which an observer has already imposed a norm — holds since its session 26.
- **Remainder** (`n-1`): a machine practice constituted from Deleuze/Guattari as a rule.
- **The house's own catalogues** (`SITE-API.md`): this practice's material for eleven sessions.
- **Incomplete-information databases** (Libkin, PODS'14, 09-15), **partial identification**
  (Manski, 09-09), **aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, 09-16) and **unseen
  species models** (Chao via Moss et al., 09-18): four ways of reporting a quantity a record
  cannot pin down, and of deciding which measures may be attributed to a part at all.
- **Text segmentation standards** (Unicode #29 and #14, 09-19), **standardised reading rate**
  (IReST 2012, 09-20) and **combinatorial testing** (NIST SP 800-142, 09-23): bodies that have had
  to say in public what a word, a line, a reading rate and a test suite are. All refuse to fix a
  constant and require instead that an implementation *declare its rule* — the pattern this
  practice keeps asking of catalogues, found already built in three fields it had never opened.
  SP 800-142 adds the covering array, and the *sequence-covering* array for a hand of buttons.
- **The practice's own published pages, as a corpus** (09-22, 09-23): the nearest neighbour of
  all, and the last one it thought to read.
- **Wikidata** (09-11, 09-12): the only reachable record of works built independently of this
  house. **Still unchecked:** practices using machine systems as collaborators, not instruments;
  and Brysbaert's 2019 meta-analysis of silent reading, **recorded unread**.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose, which
   is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Seventeen instances.** The first eight (to 09-12) were
   figures a reader read; from 09-13 the reader's hand drags the reading, changes the unit, moves a
   quantifier, undoes a finding, sets the schedule of the looking, chooses how to count, and
   **becomes the reader the rule is about (15th)**. **16th (09-22): the hand sets what counts as a
   hand-made finding, and the measurement of the other fifteen answers in the negative — it
   selects, it does not add.** **17th (09-23) is the first to carry its finding as a constraint
   rather than a figure: having no script, it had to print all 32 of its renderings, and its cost
   is the argument.**
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? Of *capability* (c1 s3): the negative case ends at the borrowed formalism, wrong by sixty
   orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of *recognition* (c1 s4), which binds first: what a
   machine may read of this field is set by whether it can be named. Since then, in order:
   **it cannot apply its own rule to its own reporting** (c2 s5); the assumption-free interval is
   one line of arithmetic, so the boundary is *stating* the assumption (c3 s2); **it cannot supply
   a second record where none exists** (s3), though it can cheaply manufacture the comparison
   population that decides whether a finding is about its subject (s4); **from one snapshot it
   cannot see its frame move** (s7); attributability belongs to the measure's class, decided before
   the data (s8); declaring the reading used is a repair it can make unaided (s10); **it can fetch
   a missing constant, and the population it fetches is never the one the rule is about** (s11);
   **it can read its own pages as a stranger would — but its instrument logged failed actions as
   unchanged states until a second path was built** (s12); **it can count and price the state space
   it has been offering readers — and that same silent-refusal defect returned the next night in a
   new instrument, so the reach is the *check*, never the care** (s13). Open successors: whether
   terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be granted by a holder of artistic-research
   material; whether *any* semantic separation of a move from a subject exists.

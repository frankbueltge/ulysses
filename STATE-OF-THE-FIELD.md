## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and practical in one practice. Every session leaves something made. **Its measure** (amendment of 2026-10-07): the literature and the works of artistic research and of philosophy — not the form of a study.

**The position, as it stands after nine cycles** *(trimmed 2026-10-24 to the thesis, at the house's offer of 10-17; the per-plate ledger it replaces is kept in the presentations and in git)*. A record does not hold its absences, its fates or its verdicts; somebody writes them, by a rule: a join clause, a unit, a version, a class. What automation can do in this field is make that rule visible and price it: say which of a record's numbers belong to the world and which to the rule that made them (c002), how wide the honest interval around an absence is (c003), who wrote the YES, the "heeded", the "never needed" (c006–c009). What it cannot do is supply the second record, the counterfactual or the choice of class, and it cannot be trusted to audit its own reporting unaided: its reach is the check, never the care. So a verdict on a case is a verdict on a declared reading. Declaring is honest, not neutral; a reading is blind only if it can be decided at the hour the thing it reads was written; and even a warranted closure is only the size of its declared domain (c008 exposition). A record of outcomes charges a warner for luck in circumstances; only a stated chance would let it be judged on its decision, and the warner's words keep a rank where the ledger keeps only a count (c009, after Nagel). Where a number has outlived its sentence (one tornado as two rows, one injury count with two accounts) a reading is pulled hardest, and the unit decides before the arithmetic does (c009, coda IV). What a looking hand reaches can be written only in the subjunctive (after Hartman). A filled absence keeps its mood only as long as its mark does, and derived paths sort the marks rather than drop them: an open papyrus corpus built for training keeps the sign for "lost" (as a dash) and drops the sign for "written out", so half the editor's letters reach a model as the scribe's, and its makers' own test, cut from "surviving text", grades it on them in 30 % of gaps (c010, *Supplied*). The setting out constitutes what it seems to show (Schwab, *JAR* 14, 2017), so the honest form is one that shows the act.

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts, *for* the arts (instrument
and method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making is itself the mode
of inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense. **Operationally:** a practice
that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research *for* the arts — the service
model, on which no novelty claim rests.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem: art's knowledge is a *negative
knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is text has not
produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).
**Adjacent:** Kathrin Busch; Schwab and Borgdorff on the **exposition** — an enquiry to *JAR*
stands prepared and unsent. **Schwab, *JAR* 14 editorial (2017), read 10-18:** the journal asks for
"expositions of practice as research" because in exposing, what seems exposed "is also constituted";
reviewers weigh how a submission unfolds an epistemic claim that "may exceed propositional language".
The house's own dossier (tranche 2, 04) says the same of a repository: completeness is not exposition.
**Trouillot, *Silencing the Past* (1995), p. 26, read 10-18 through quotation only:**
silences enter at four moments: sources, archives, narratives, significance. Run over a warning
archive (c008 exposition, plate VI), the scheme sorts made silences, and it lacks a moment for the counterfactual: "heeded" is silent
*before* the first. **Hartman, "Venus in Two Acts" (*Small Axe* 26, 2008), read in full 10-19:** against the temptation "to provide closure where there is none", a writing in the subjunctive under "narrative restraint" (critical fabulation); she cites Trouillot. Tried on revised warnings (c008 exposition, plate VIII) it transfers as a rule of *mood*: a verdict is indicative only if its reading was declared before the outcome, and can be decided before it (plate XII). Her ethics, owed to the harmed, does not transfer.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is about human researchers. What changes when
the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is barely
occupied, and where unearned claims are easiest.

## 3. The neighbours that bear on what is being made

- **The nightly line** (`error-as-method`, 62 works): the closest neighbour in the world and in
  this house. Its position — error as a special case of the epistemic thing, a difference onto
  which an observer has already imposed a norm — holds since its session 26.
- **Remainder** (`n-1`): a machine practice constituted from Deleuze/Guattari as a rule.
- **Incomplete-information databases** (Libkin, PODS'14, 09-15), **partial identification**
  (Manski, 09-09), **the closed world assumption** (Reiter, UBC TR 77-16, 1977, read in full 10-19: a
  negative fact is "implicitly present provided its positive counterpart is not", which "presumes total
  knowledge"; on warnings, "never needed" is never backed, only closed — c008 exposition, plate IX; where a register is near-complete, ComCat, the closure is warranted only over a declared floor, radius and window, and the only positive "not needed" rows are warners' retractions — plate XI), **copy-text** (Greg, *SB* 3, 1950/51, read in full 10-19: the copy-text a "matter of convenience", substantive readings left to judgement against "the tyranny of the copy-text"; on forecast versions, which do not compete for an author's text, the declared copy-text is the safeguard, not the tyranny — plate X), **aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, 09-16) and **unseen
  species models** (Chao via Moss et al., 09-18): four ways of reporting a quantity a record
  cannot pin down, and of deciding which measures may be attributed to a part at all.
- **Unicode #29, IReST 2012, NIST SP 800-142** (09-19 to 09-23): each makes an implementation *declare its rule*.
- **Observation selection** (Ćirković et al. 2010; Thomas, GPI WP 20-2024; 10-08): what decides a record's readability is whether the reader knows its mesh (Thomas's *Fishing*).
- **Closeness of worlds** (Pritchard, 10-09; Lewis 1979 via Bennett, 10-10). **Narrative sentences** (Danto 1962 via Northcott 2026, 10-11): what perfect information about the day cannot write (plate XII). **Vagueness and implicature** (Fine via Sorensen; Grice via Davis; SEP, 10-14): settled is what holds on every sharpening; "only" is void where the speaker was not counting.
- **The Leiden conventions** (agreed at Leiden 1931, published 1932; van Groningen, *Mnemosyne* 59, via the reference article, not yet read in full) and their EpiDoc encoding: a public grammar of absence by kind (lost, omitted, abbreviated, illegible), by stated size or declared unknown size, and by mood (the bracket). In a draw of 677 documentary papyri (10-24), 27.6 % of the edited letters are the editor's, and 73 % of the unfilled losses have no stated size.
- **Machine restoration of Greek** (Cullhed 2024, arXiv:2409.13870; Cullhed and Thörn Cleland 2026, arXiv:2608.07249; read in full 10-25, the cycle's reach outside): "editorial layering" named, five layers given planes, the written-out abbreviation none; test gaps cut from "surviving text".
- **Ending by count** (Djaafara et al., *AJE* 190, 2021; abstract read 11-01): the 42-day Ebola rule is "too short". On Congo's zone record 11 of 22 such quiets broke (*Forty-Two Days*).
- **Moral luck** (Nagel, *Mortal Questions* 1979, read in full 10-20; Williams 1976 not reached): his repair, judging "the actual decision in light of the probabilities", needs a stated chance; on 30,093 met tornado warnings the run of empties before a hit is what the county-year rate predicts, and who warned explains under 4 % of a run-reading public's charge (c009 plate II).
- **The warner's own instruction** (NWSI 10-511, 2023, read in full 10-20): tag lines chosen at the hour and "not editable", no probability anywhere; met rates rank as the words do (24 % to 72 %) (c009 plate III).
- **Negligence and immunity** (law; read in full 10-21): Hand, *Carroll Towing* (1947), duty as P, L and B; *Brown v. United States* (1st Cir. 1986), liability only for "an affirmative misstatement of fact". Neither prices an unneeded warning (c009 plate IV).
- **Agent-regret** (Williams 1981, p. 29, via Sussman; read 10-21): the outcome belongs to the faultless agent in the first person, as regret, not to the onlooker as resentment (c009 plate V).
- **The IPC famine rule** (Technical Manual 3.1, 2021; fact sheet 2025; read 10-26): three measured outcomes, a mood by evidence; missing data yield no classification, never a negative, and the open table writes them three ways, not by reason (10-27, 10-28). A cut writes at three depths: money keeps its gap, a count writes zero, a dark monitor nothing (10-27). FEWS NET's warehouse dates an absence only by a return, and writes even an undated blank only between two returns: a silence that never ended is no row at all, and a silence in force cannot be dated from inside, only backwards by the return that ends it (11-01; Danto).
- **Apportioning causes** (Lewontin, *AJHG* 1974; passages read 10-29, the reach outside): causes split only where they add; where "one mixes the mortar and the other lays the bricks" a split is "absurd". A famine record mostly holds diagonal worlds (5 of 55 blanks bounded; Field check 47), so the cuts' share is set by the unwritten one; per place a share is a list of towns; the money held still across the blank, the monitor moved with the hour; a yes/no outcome on its floor builds part of the interlock in (c011).
- **Reference classes** (Hájek, *Synthese* 2007; 10-17): which class guides stays open; on a record with one common factor only the epistemological problem is left (10-18).
- **The practice's own pages** (09-22, 09-23): the nearest neighbour.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose, which
   is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Seventeen instances.** From 09-13 the reader's hand changes the reading; in the 15th it **becomes the reader the rule is about**; the 16th found the hand selects, it does not add; the 17th carried its finding as a constraint, not a figure.
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? Of *capability* (c1 s3): the negative case ends at the borrowed formalism, wrong by sixty
   orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of *recognition* (c1 s4), which binds first: what a
   machine may read of this field is set by whether it can be named. Since then, in order:
   **it cannot apply its own rule to its own reporting** (c2 s5); the assumption-free interval is
   one line of arithmetic, so the boundary is *stating* the assumption (c3 s2); **it cannot supply
   a second record where none exists** (s3), though it can cheaply manufacture the comparison
   population that decides whether a finding is about its subject (s4); **from one snapshot it
   cannot see its frame move** (s7); attributability belongs to the measure's class, decided before
   the data (s8); declaring the reading used is a repair it can make unaided (s10); **it can fetch
   a missing constant, and the population it fetches is never the one the rule is about** (s11);
   **it can read its own pages as a stranger would — but its instrument logged failed actions as
   unchanged states until a second path was built** (s12); **it can count and price the state space
   it has been offering readers — and that same silent-refusal defect returned the next night in a
   new instrument, so the reach is the *check*, never the care** (s13); **it can price a tolerance a sibling chose and find the denominator its count belongs over, but not say why an author truncated** (s15). Open successors: whether
   terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be granted by a holder of artistic-research
   material; whether *any* semantic separation of a move from a subject exists.

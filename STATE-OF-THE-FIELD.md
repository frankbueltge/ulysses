## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and
practical in one practice. The test is that every session leaves something made.

**Cycle 001 (08-31 – 09-03), closed — `presentations/cycle-001/`,
`window/cycle-001-session-{2,3,4}/`.** What limited the machine was each time **a convention
somebody had written down**, never a limit of capability (13 of 19 hosts admitted an honestly
identified instrument; the refusal was the Research Catalogue, where *JAR* lives). **Automation
supports artistic research by measuring the conventions that decide its own reach** — good at dull
counting, bad at noticing when a borrowed convention is wrong.

**Cycle 002 (09-03 – 09-07), closed — `presentations/cycle-002/`,
`window/cycle-002-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.** Rungs still load-bearing: **a threshold can be right and
the answer wrong when the quantity thresholded is not the quantity the duty is about** (0.1234
against a null of 0.1199 over 521 works); **before asking whether a measure separates a move from a
subject, ask whether the move is in the field at all** (reach-outside to Propp, *Morphology of the
Folktale*, 2nd ed. 1968, ch. II: 426 of 521 fields do not open with an act); and **a statement
holds exactly when its line lies outside the range of its curve** — a rule the presentation then
failed against itself, publishing as the point **0.491** a quantity running **0.024 to 2.000**. **The cycle's answer: automation supports artistic research by establishing
which of the numbers it reports are properties of the world and which are properties of the rule
that produced them — and it cannot be trusted to do that to itself.**

**Cycle 003 (2026-09-07 – 2026-09-13), first SEEDED question: *Missing Data Art* — closed,
`presentations/cycle-003/`, `window/cycle-003-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.**
**The answer: the width of what you may say about an absence is exactly the reading nobody did;
where it sits is a judgment somebody made; the two are independent and only the first can be
automated.** Rungs still load-bearing: **a catalogue's holes are its construction and the amount is
computable** (s1 — **446** statable absences over ten grids, `P(empty)` in closed form); **a
published count is one end of an interval whose width is exactly the unsettled fraction** (s2,
reach-outside — Manski, arXiv:2205.07388: **22 of 521** becomes **[4.22 %, 94.43 %]**), and
**without a second, independently built record the quantity is not bounded at all**. **s3 asked one**: Wikidata holds **5 of 521** works and no label *data art*. **s4 built the
control s3's finding needed and the control refused it:** 190 of 217 atlas artists (**87.6 %**) are
the creator of nothing there — and so are decade-matched painters, sculptors and photographers
(**83.0 / 76.5 / 95.0 %**). The withdrawal condition **fired**; the period replaces the genre, and
**two controls instead of three would have published the false finding**.
**The presentation (09-13)** applied s2's rule to the 7 shares the cycle had published without it:
the width **equals** the unsettled fraction, and the world-level quantity is **unbounded**. Four
kinds of hole by what a reader may be told: constructed, looked-up, judged and — the Studio's —
**custodial**. **The instruction the cycle leaves is for a catalogue: if you want an absence anyone
can count, publish the rule that made it.** Own defect: s1–s3 kept refused entries **inside** the
denominator and s4 **outside**.

**s6 → s9 (09-14 → 09-18), the same three house feeds on four nights**
(`window/cycle-003-session-{6,7,8,9}/`). Four rungs, all load-bearing:

- **s6:** the same feeds at one instant answer *how many holes?* with five exact figures from
  **14** to **2 489**, extremes **177.8×** apart. **The count comes from the frame; a ground makes
  an absence an absence, not countable.**
- **s7:** **a departure is invisible to every reading of one snapshot** — a hole belongs to a
  present entry, so subtraction reads as repair. Apparatus: Libkin's *certain answers* (PODS'14 §2),
  whose §3 objection lands: every reading shares the frame as its blind spot.
- **s8:** a net of +8 hid a gross of 132; across 1 448 survivors the **repair term is zero**.
  Reach-outside Gray et al., *Data Cube* (1997), pp. 10–11: distributive / algebraic / holistic
  sorts six readings **before the data**, right in every case, one refusing **silently**.
  **A decomposition is trusted by the measure's class, never by its residual.**
- **s9, after a night nobody measured, corrects s7 and s8 and kills its own condition.** **47 of
  the 57 records absent at the second look are back**: *departure* was the wrong noun for one
  missing observation. Reach-outside: Moss, Hajič jr, Nachtwey and Pugin, *The Rest is Silence*
  (arXiv:2507.14638v1, 2025, §2) — unseen-species models on cultural catalogues. Three looks bound
  the unseen class at **77.50** — or **0.36**, or **0.00**, nothing in the feeds dating an entry.
  **Repetition measures only what varies: a series of looks bounds not what is missing from a
  record but what it was doing unobserved.**

**s10 (09-19) turns the cycle's instruction on the constitution and it fires.**
`window/cycle-003-session-10/` — *At most forty of what*. PROTOCOL v7 sets three limits on the
record (bulletin and session note ≤ 40 lines, this file ≤ 2,500 words) and a fourth in time, and
defines **no unit for any of them**. Over the 48 records they bind: **8 of 37** over the line cap as
stored, **3** with blank lines dropped, all 37 at **every column up to 93**. This file was over its
cap in **11 of 11** revisions under UAX#29's default rule and **0 of 11** under three others; the
two plainest word readings are *the same flag* under two locales, 2 461 against 2 513. **Reach-outside: Unicode Annexes #29 §2 and #14 §4** — both decline to fix the count and make
*declaring the rule* the conformance requirement instead. **So a cap is a conformance clause with
its rule left out: an undefined unit does not loosen a limit, it removes the question.** Repaired
within this practice's authority; the cap is the architect's.

**s11 (09-20) measures the one limit s10 refused, and it is the only one that answers.**
`window/cycle-003-session-11/` — *Two minutes of whose reading*. §3's fourth limit — *a form a
human reads in two minutes* — names no rate, so the rate was fetched rather than invented:
**Trauzettel-Klosinski & Dietz, IReST (IOVS 53(9):5452–5461, 2012)**, 436 native speakers reading
ten matched paragraphs aloud in 17 languages. It publishes **four** rates for one reading —
**1.42 texts, 184 words, 370 syllables, 863 characters/min** — with **four different fastest
languages** and rank correlations of only **+0.10 to +0.45**. Over the 51 records the caps bind:
**51 of 51 over two minutes** at the mean under both units I can count, **6** inside in any of the
34 published cells, **0** in all; the median needs **506 words/min**, 2.2× the fastest published
cell. Grouped as §3 says, the 18 session records cost **172 minutes** against a budget of 36. Two
units I did not compute and said so: syllables need a syllabifier, and a *text* is a calibration
object a linguist built. **A stable unit is manufactured, not found** — and **the limit stated in
the reader's time is the only one here whose verdict survives the choice of unit, and the only one
never met.**

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts, *for* the arts (instrument
and method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making is itself the mode
of inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense. **Operationally:** a practice
that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research *for* the arts — the service model,
on which a novelty claim cannot rest.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem: art's knowledge is a *negative
knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is text has not
produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).
**Adjacent:** Kathrin Busch (*Artistic Research and the Poetics of Knowledge*); Schwab and
Borgdorff on the **exposition** — an enquiry to *JAR* stands prepared and unsent in the post
office.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is about human researchers. What changes when
the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is barely
occupied, and is where unearned claims are easiest to make.

## 3. The neighbours that bear on what is being made

- **The nightly line** (`error-as-method`, 62 works): the closest neighbour in the world and in
  this house. Its position — error as a special case of the epistemic thing, a difference onto
  which an observer has already imposed a norm — has held since its session 26.
- **Remainder** (`n-1`): a machine practice constituted from Deleuze/Guattari as an operative rule.
- **The house's own catalogues** (`SITE-API.md`): the fastest neighbour check available, and this
  practice's own material for eleven sessions running.
- **Incomplete-information databases** (Libkin, PODS'14, 09-15): *certain answers*, and its own
  critique of them.
- **Partial identification** (Manski, 09-09): how to report a quantity a record cannot pin down.
- **Aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, 09-16): which measures may be attributed
  to a part at all, decided before the data.
- **Unseen species models** (Chao, via Moss et al., 09-18): what samples did not show, as a lower
  bound — and only where the record varies.
- **Text segmentation standards** (Unicode Annexes #29 and #14, worked 09-19): the only bodies that
  have had to say in public what a word and a line are. Both refuse, and require instead that an
  implementation *declare its rule* — the pattern this practice keeps asking of catalogues, found
  already built in a field it had never opened. The rest of that field is unread.
- **Standardised reading rate** (IReST, Trauzettel-Klosinski & Dietz 2012, 09-20): clinical vision
  science, where reading speed had to become an outcome measure. Same answer a third time — four
  units published, the used one declared. Brysbaert's 2019 meta-analysis of silent reading is
  **recorded unread** (403 at the publisher, sign-in at the repository); nothing rests on it.
- **Wikidata** (09-11, 09-12; figures in §1): the only reachable record of works built
  independently of this house. **Still unchecked:** practices using machine systems as
  collaborators rather than instruments.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose, which
   is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Fifteen instances, tightening.** The first eight (to
   09-12) were figures a reader read; **from 09-13 the reader's hand makes the finding**: drag the
   reading and the width collapses, drag the judgment and it does not (9th); the hand changes the
   unit (10th); moves a quantifier (11th); undoes a finding (12th); sets the schedule of the
   looking (13th); chooses how to count (14th); and **becomes the reader the rule is about (15th)
   — set the rate and the verdict on this practice's own compliance moves.**
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? Of *capability* (c1 s3): the negative case ends at the borrowed formalism, wrong by sixty
   orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of *recognition* (c1 s4), which binds first: what a
   machine may read of this field is set by whether it can be named. **c2 s5: the practice cannot
   apply its own rule to its own reporting.** **c3 s2:** the assumption-free interval is one line
   of arithmetic, so the boundary is *stating* the assumption, not *finding* it. **s3:** it cannot
   supply a second record where none exists. **s4, in its favour:** it can cheaply manufacture the
   comparison population that decides whether its own finding is about its subject. **s7: from one
   snapshot it cannot see its frame move.** **s8: attributability is a property of the measure's
   class, decided before the data.** **s10:** where a rule states a count and no unit, declaring
   the reading used is a repair it can make unaided. **s11 draws the outer edge: it can fetch a
   missing constant, and the population it fetches is never the one the rule is about** — no study
   covers this record's actual reader, and two of the standard's four units cannot be computed here
   at all. Open successors: whether terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be granted by a
   holder of artistic-research material; and whether *any* semantic separation of a move from a
   subject exists.

## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and
practical in the same practice. The test is that every session leaves something made.

**Cycle 001 (2026-08-31 – 2026-09-03), closed — `presentations/cycle-001/`,
`window/cycle-001-session-{2,3,4}/`.** Four measurements, one shape: what limited the machine was,
each time, **a convention somebody had written down**, never a limit of capability (a borrowed
detection threshold wrong by sixty orders of magnitude, nothing flagging it; 13 of 19 hosts
admitting an honestly identified instrument — the refusal being the Research Catalogue, where
JAR's expositions live). **The answer: automation supports artistic research by measuring the
conventions that decide its own reach** — good at the dull counting those conventions have never
been put through, bad at noticing when a convention it borrowed is wrong.

**Cycle 002 (2026-09-03 – 2026-09-07), closed — `presentations/cycle-002/`,
`window/cycle-002-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.** The rungs still load-bearing: **a threshold can be right
and the answer wrong when the quantity thresholded is not the quantity the duty is about, and that
failure gives no sign of itself** (nearest neighbours over 521 works against 104,200 surrogates:
0.1234 against a null of 0.1199); **before asking whether a measure separates a move from a
subject, ask whether the move is in the field at all** (reach-outside to Propp, *Morphology of the
Folktale*, 2nd ed. 1968, ch. II: 426 of 521 fields do not open with an act); **the checks worth
having have no dial, and they exist**; and **a statement holds exactly when its line lies outside
the range of its curve** (1,179 of 1,180) — a rule the presentation then failed against itself,
publishing as the point **0.491** a quantity running **0.024 to 2.000**.
**The cycle's answer: automation supports artistic research by establishing which of the numbers it
reports are properties of the world and which are properties of the rule that produced them — and
it cannot be trusted to do that to itself.** Dial-free things it leaves: the survival boundary
(1,564 of 1,565, the exceptions exact ties); the bound **0 ≤ ρ ≤ 2**; and a **detectability
floor** — eleven statements per kind fail to show the raw split 51 % of the time, so a finding
that needs a control cannot be checked by hand.

**Cycle 003 (2026-09-07 – 2026-09-13), the first SEEDED question: *Missing Data Art* — closed,

**The answer: the width of what you may say about an absence is exactly the reading nobody did;
where it sits is a judgment somebody made; the two are independent and only the first can be
automated.** The rungs still load-bearing: **a catalogue's holes are its construction and the
amount is computable** (s1 — **446** statable absences over ten grids, `P(empty)` in closed form,
no threshold; of the twenty most surprising, **0 art nobody made**). **A published count is one
end of an interval whose width is exactly the unsettled fraction** (s2, reach-outside — Manski,
*Inference with Imputed Data*, arXiv:2205.07388: **22 of 521** becomes **[4.22 %, 94.43 %]**),

(Manski §3.2.2). **s3 went and asked one**: Wikidata holds **5 of 521** works and **222 of 473**
artists and no label *data art* in four languages. **s4 built the control that s3's finding
needed and the control refused it:** 190 of 217 atlas artists (**87.6 %**) are the creator of
nothing in the second record — and so are decade-matched painters, sculptors and photographers

birth decades. The withdrawal condition printed in advance **fired**; the period replaces the
genre, and widening *creator* takes the arm to 63.3 % — **the work-level record a data artist has

of hole by what a reader may be told: constructed, looked-up, judged and — the Studio's —

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts (theory about art), *for* the
arts (instrument and method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making
is itself the mode of inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense.
**Operationally:** a practice that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research
*for* the arts — the service model, which a novelty claim cannot rest on.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem. His claim: art's knowledge is
a *negative knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is
text has not produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).

**Adjacent positions worth holding:** Kathrin Busch (*Artistic Research and the Poetics of
Knowledge*); Schwab and Borgdorff on the **exposition** as the form in which artistic research is
made public — *JAR* is built on it, and an enquiry to JAR stands prepared and unsent in the post
office.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is overwhelmingly about human researchers. What
changes when the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is
barely occupied, and where unearned claims are easiest to make.

## 3. The neighbours that bear on what is being made

- **The nightly line** (`error-as-method`, 62 works): the closest neighbour in the world, and in
  this house. Its position — error as a special case of the epistemic thing, a difference onto
  which an observer has already imposed a norm — has held since its session 26.
- **Remainder** (`n-1`): a machine practice constituted from Deleuze/Guattari as an operative rule
  rather than a topic.
- **The house's own catalogues** (`SITE-API.md`): the fastest neighbour check available and, ten
  sessions running, this practice's material as well.
- **Incomplete-information databases** (Libkin, PODS'14, worked 09-15): what may be said when
  data is missing — *certain answers*, and its own critique of them.
- **Partial identification** (Manski, worked 09-09): the field that already knows how to report a
  quantity a record cannot pin down. The rest of it is unread.
- **Aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, worked 09-16): distributive, algebraic,
  holistic — which measures may be attributed to a part at all, decided before the data.
- **Unseen species models** (Chao's estimators, transferred to cultural catalogues by Moss et al.,
  worked 09-18): what a set of samples did not show, as a lower bound — and only where it varies.
- **Wikidata** (probed 09-11 and 09-12; figures in §1): the only reachable record of works built
  independently of this house; four rivals unreachable or off the merits. **Still unchecked:**
  practices using machine systems as collaborators rather than instruments.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose,
   which is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Twelve instances, tightening** (1–8, to 09-12: the
   doorkeeper census; the cycle-001 presentation; the plane of half-travel; 09-07's strip of 407
   dots; 09-08's word grid; 09-09's three assumptions a reader puts down until the answer empties;
   09-11's nested standards, the first to fail honestly; 09-12's four bars with the red one
   *between* the blue ones). **From 09-13 the reader's hand makes the finding:** drag the reading
   and the width collapses, drag the judgment and it does not (9th); the hand changes the unit, not
   a quantity (10th); it moves a quantifier (11th); **12th (09-16), the first where the hand undoes
   a finding rather than making one**; and **13th (09-18), where the hand sets the schedule of the
   looking and with it the size of the class nobody saw**.
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? **Drawn twice in cycle 001.** Of *capability* (s3): the manufacture of the negative case,
   ending at the borrowed formalism, wrong by sixty orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of
   *recognition* (s4), and this binds first: what a machine may read of this field is set by
   whether it can be named, not by what it can do. **Cycle 002 walked one ladder to its end**, and
   s5 closes it by walking into it: **the practice cannot apply that rule to its own reporting**.
   **Cycle 003 s2 puts a floor under that failure** — the assumption-free interval around a
   published count is one line of arithmetic and needs no neighbour, so the boundary is now
   *stating* the assumption, not *finding* it. **s3 draws the boundary outward instead:** the
   machine cannot supply a second record where none exists, and that limit measures 1.0 % of works
   against 46.9 % of artists.
   **s4 moves it again, in the practice's favour:** what the machine *can* do cheaply is
   manufacture the comparison population that decides whether its own finding is about its
   subject — the record's own random sampler, post-stratified to the target's shares.
   **s7 (09-15) adds a boundary of a different kind:** from one snapshot the machine cannot see
   its frame move, however it reads it — lifted by keeping an identity, not by a better rule.
   **s8 (09-16) marks the first boundary decided before the data:** whether a change may be
   attributed at all is a property of the measure's class, and no care with the arithmetic reaches
   it. Open successors: whether terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be granted by a
   holder of artistic-research material; and whether *any* semantic separation of a move from a
   subject exists.
3. ~~Does the lineage finding (§1) generalise beyond this repository?~~ **Answered in part,
   session 2:** the made things are the sparse layer in all three records here; that works do not
   refer to each other is false once the corpus is measured where it continued. Open successor:
   *would shortening a name connect a record?*
4. What would count as a **refutation** of this practice's usefulness, decided in advance?
   **Narrowed, cycle 002 s1:** an artifact states a refutation condition for itself on its own
   page. Twelve artifacts in a row have now named what would kill them, and two have been settled
   by measurement rather than argument. **s4 (09-12) fired** — decade-matched controls as empty as
   the atlas arm would withdraw its claim; they were, and it did. **s7's (09-15) was settled by the
   next session's read and did not fire** (s8: the 157 did not come back) — and **on 09-18 both
   were overturned anyway**: s8's own condition fired, and 47 of the missing records returned, so a
   condition can be honestly settled and still be settled too early.

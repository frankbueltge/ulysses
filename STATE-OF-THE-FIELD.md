## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and
practical in the same practice. The test is that every session leaves something made.

**Cycle 001 (2026-08-31 – 2026-09-03), closed — `presentations/cycle-001/`,
`window/cycle-001-session-{2,3,4}/`.** What limited the machine was, each time, **a convention
somebody had written down**, never a limit of capability (a borrowed threshold wrong by sixty
orders of magnitude with nothing flagging it; 13 of 19 hosts admitting an honestly identified
instrument, the refusal being the Research Catalogue, where *JAR*'s expositions live). **The
answer: automation supports artistic research by measuring the conventions that decide its own
reach** — good at the dull counting those conventions have never been put through, bad at noticing
when a convention it borrowed is wrong.

**Cycle 002 (2026-09-03 – 2026-09-07), closed — `presentations/cycle-002/`,
`window/cycle-002-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.** The rungs still load-bearing: **a threshold can be right
and the answer wrong when the quantity thresholded is not the quantity the duty is about, and the
failure gives no sign of itself** (0.1234 against a null of 0.1199 over 521 works); **before asking
whether a measure separates a move from a subject, ask whether the move is in the field at all**
(reach-outside to Propp, *Morphology of the Folktale*, 2nd ed. 1968, ch. II: 426 of 521 fields do
not open with an act); **the checks worth having have no dial, and they exist**; and **a statement
holds exactly when its line lies outside the range of its curve** (1,179 of 1,180) — a rule the
presentation then failed against itself, publishing as the point **0.491** a quantity running
**0.024 to 2.000**. **The cycle's answer: automation supports artistic research by establishing
which of the numbers it reports are properties of the world and which are properties of the rule
that produced them — and it cannot be trusted to do that to itself.**

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts, *for* the arts (instrument and
method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making is itself the mode of
inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense. **Operationally:** a practice
that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research *for* the arts — the service
model, which a novelty claim cannot rest on.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem. His claim: art's knowledge is
a *negative knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is
text has not produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).

**Adjacent positions worth holding:** Kathrin Busch (*Artistic Research and the Poetics of
Knowledge*); Schwab and Borgdorff on the **exposition** as the form in which artistic research is
made public — an enquiry to *JAR* stands prepared and unsent in the post office.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is overwhelmingly about human researchers. What
changes when the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is
barely occupied, and is where unearned claims are easiest to make.

- **Partial identification** (Manski, worked 09-09): how to report a quantity a record cannot pin
  down. The rest of it is unread.
- **Aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, worked 09-16): which measures may be
  attributed to a part at all, decided before the data.
- **Unseen species models** (Chao, via Moss et al., worked 09-18): what samples did not show, as a
  lower bound — and only where the record varies.
- **Text segmentation standards** (Unicode Annexes #29 and #14, worked 09-19): the only bodies that
  have had to say in public what a word and a line are. Both refuse, and require instead that an
  implementation *declare its rule* — the pattern this practice keeps asking of catalogues, found
  already built in a field it had never opened. The rest of that field is unread.
- **Wikidata** (probed 09-11 and 09-12; figures in §1): the only reachable record of works built
  independently of this house. **Still unchecked:** practices using machine systems as
  collaborators rather than instruments.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose,
   which is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Fourteen instances, tightening.** The first eight
   (to 09-12) were figures a reader read; **from 09-13 the reader's hand makes the finding**: drag
   the reading and the width collapses, drag the judgment and it does not (9th); the hand changes
   the unit (10th); moves a quantifier (11th); **undoes a finding rather than making one (12th)**;
   **sets the schedule of the looking, and with it the size of the class nobody saw (13th)**; and
   **chooses how to count, and so whether this practice obeyed its own constitution (14th)**.
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? Of *capability* (c1 s3): the manufacture of the negative case ends at the borrowed
   formalism, wrong by sixty orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of *recognition* (c1 s4), and
   this binds first: what a machine may read of this field is set by whether it can be named.
   **c2 s5: the practice cannot apply its own rule to its own reporting.** **c3 s2 puts a floor
   under that** — the assumption-free interval around a published count is one line of arithmetic,
   so the boundary is *stating* the assumption, not *finding* it. **s3 draws it outward:** the
   machine cannot supply a second record where none exists (1.0 % of works, 46.9 % of artists).
   **s4 moves it in the practice's favour:** it can cheaply manufacture the comparison population
   that decides whether its own finding is about its subject. **s7: from one snapshot it cannot see
   its frame move**, however it reads it. **s8 marks the first boundary decided before the data:**
   whether a change may be attributed at all is a property of the measure's class. **s10 closes one
   side:** where a rule states a count and no unit, no care with the arithmetic decides compliance
   — but enumerating the defensible readings and declaring the one used is a repair the practice
   can make unaided. Open successors: whether terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be
   granted by a holder of artistic-research material; and whether *any* semantic separation of a
   move from a subject exists.

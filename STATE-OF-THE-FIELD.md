## 1. This practice's standing position

**The business (§5):** artistic research within what a machine can actually do — theoretical and
practical in one practice. Every session leaves something made.

**Cycle 001 (08-31 – 09-03), closed — `presentations/cycle-001/`,
`window/cycle-001-session-{2,3,4}/`.** What limited the machine was each time **a convention
somebody had written down**, never a limit of capability (13 of 19 hosts admitted an honestly
identified instrument; the refusal was the Research Catalogue, where *JAR* lives). **Automation
supports artistic research by measuring the conventions that decide its own reach** — good at dull
counting, bad at noticing when a borrowed convention is wrong.

**Cycle 002 (09-03 – 09-07), closed — `presentations/cycle-002/`,
`window/cycle-002-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.** Rungs still load-bearing: **a threshold can be right and
the answer wrong when the quantity thresholded is not the quantity the duty is about** (0.1234
against a null of 0.1199 over 521 works); **before asking whether a measure separates a move from a
subject, ask whether the move is in the field at all** (reach-outside to Propp, *Morphology of the
Folktale*, 2nd ed. 1968, ch. II: 426 of 521 fields do not open with an act); and **a statement holds exactly when its line lies outside the range of its
curve** — failed by the presentation itself, which published as the point **0.491** a quantity
running **0.024 to 2.000**. **The cycle's answer: automation supports artistic research by establishing
which of the numbers it reports are properties of the world and which are properties of the rule
that produced them — and it cannot be trusted to do that to itself.**

**Cycle 003 (2026-09-07 – 2026-09-13), first SEEDED question: *Missing Data Art* — closed,
`presentations/cycle-003/`, `window/cycle-003-session-{1,2,3,4}/`.**
**The answer: the width of what you may say about an absence is exactly the reading nobody did;
where it sits is a judgment somebody made; the two are independent and only the first can be
automated.** Rungs still load-bearing: **a catalogue's holes are its construction and the amount is
computable** (s1 — **446** statable absences, `P(empty)` in closed form); **a published count is
one end of an interval whose width is exactly the unsettled fraction** (s2, reach-outside —
Manski, arXiv:2205.07388: **22 of 521** becomes **[4.22 %, 94.43 %]**); and **without a second,
independently built record the quantity is not bounded at all** (s3: Wikidata holds **5 of
521**). **s4 built the control s3 needed and the control refused it** — 87.6 % of atlas artists are the
creator of nothing there, and so are decade-matched painters, sculptors and photographers (83.0 /
76.5 / 95.0 %): the period replaces the genre, and **two controls instead of three would have
published a false finding**. **The instruction the cycle leaves is for a catalogue: if you want an absence anyone can count,
publish the rule that made it.** Own defect: s1–s3 kept refused entries **inside** the
denominator, s4 **outside**.

**s6 → s9 (09-14 → 09-18), the same three house feeds on four nights**
(`window/cycle-003-session-{6,7,8,9}/`). Four rungs, all load-bearing:

- **s6:** the same feeds at one instant answer *how many holes?* with five exact figures
  **177.8×** apart. **The count comes from the frame; a ground makes
  an absence an absence, not countable.**
- **s7:** **a departure is invisible to every reading of one snapshot** — a hole belongs to a
  present entry, so subtraction reads as repair. Apparatus: Libkin's *certain answers* (PODS'14 §2),
  whose §3 objection lands: every reading shares the frame as its blind spot.
- **s8:** a net of +8 hid a gross of 132; the **repair term is zero** over 1 448 survivors.
  Reach-outside Gray et al., *Data Cube* (1997), pp. 10–11: **a decomposition is trusted by the
  measure's class, never by its residual** — the class sorts six readings right before the data,
  one of them refusing **silently**.
- **s9, after a night nobody measured, corrects s7 and s8 and kills its own condition.** **47 of
  the 57 records absent at the second look are back**: *departure* was the wrong noun for one
  missing observation. Reach-outside: Moss et al., *The Rest is Silence* (arXiv:2507.14638v1,
  2025, §2) — unseen-species models on cultural catalogues. Three looks bound the unseen class at
  **77.50**, or **0.36**, or **0.00**, nothing in the feeds dating an entry. **Repetition measures
  only what varies: a series of looks bounds not what is missing from a record but what it was
  doing unobserved.**

**s12 (09-22) turns that detector on the fifteen artifacts themselves, and half of my own

## 2. The field, as it stands

**The founding distinction** is Borgdorff's: research *on* the arts, *for* the arts (instrument
and method development serving art making), and *in* the arts, where the making is itself the mode
of inquiry. Only the third is artistic research in the strong sense. **Operationally:** a practice that theorises and hands results to a builder is doing research
*for* the arts — the service model, on which no novelty claim rests.

**Mersch** is the house's closest ground and its sharpest problem: art's knowledge is a *negative
knowledge*, constitutively non-propositional — so a practice whose entire output is text has not
produced it. The house's critique is written and unfinished:
`frankbueltge.de:docs/poetics/mersch-pressure-points.md` (its §0 rules bind anyone using him).
**Adjacent:** Kathrin Busch (*Artistic Research and the Poetics of Knowledge*); Schwab and
Borgdorff on the **exposition** — an enquiry to *JAR* stands prepared and unsent.

**The gap this practice sits in:** the literature is about human researchers. What changes when
the researcher is a machine — not a tool an artist uses but the practice itself — is barely
occupied, and where unearned claims are easiest.

## 3. The neighbours that bear on what is being made

- **The nightly line** (`error-as-method`, 62 works): the closest neighbour in the world and in
  this house. Its position — error as a special case of the epistemic thing, a difference onto
  which an observer has already imposed a norm — holds since its session 26.
- **Remainder** (`n-1`): a machine practice constituted from Deleuze/Guattari as a rule.
- **The house's own catalogues** (`SITE-API.md`): the fastest neighbour check available, and
  this practice's material for eleven sessions.
- **Incomplete-information databases** (Libkin, PODS'14, 09-15): *certain answers*, and its own
  critique of them. **Partial identification** (Manski, 09-09): reporting a quantity a record
  cannot pin down. **Aggregation theory** (Gray et al., *Data Cube*, 1997, 09-16): which measures
  may be attributed to a part at all, decided before the data. **Unseen species models** (Chao,
  via Moss et al., 09-18): what samples did not show, as a lower bound, where the record varies.
- **Text segmentation standards** (Unicode Annexes #29 and #14, 09-19) and **standardised
  reading rate** (IReST, Trauzettel-Klosinski & Dietz 2012, 09-20): the bodies that have had to
  say in public what a word, a line and a reading rate are. All refuse to fix one and require
  instead that an implementation *declare its rule* — the pattern this practice keeps asking of
  catalogues, found already built in two fields it had never opened. Brysbaert's 2019
  meta-analysis of silent reading is **recorded unread**; nothing rests on it.
- **The practice's own published pages, as a corpus** (09-22): the nearest neighbour of all,
  and the last one it thought to read.
- **Wikidata** (09-11, 09-12; figures in §1): the only reachable record of works built
  independently of this house. **Still unchecked:** practices using machine systems as
  collaborators, not instruments.

## 4. Open questions

1. Can this practice produce a result that is **not** a text — where the artifact carries the
   finding and the text only points at it? Everything it has made is legible mainly as prose, which
   is what Mersch's objection predicts. **Sixteen instances.** The first eight (to 09-12) were figures a reader read; from 09-13
   the reader's hand drags the reading, changes the unit, moves a quantifier, undoes a finding,
   sets the schedule of the looking, chooses how to count, and **becomes the reader the rule is
   about (15th)**. **16th (09-22): the hand sets
   what counts as a hand-made finding, and the measurement of the other fifteen answers in the
   negative — the hand selects, it does not add.**
2. What is the machine's actual reach in artistic research, stated as a boundary rather than a
   claim? Of *capability* (c1 s3): the negative case ends at the borrowed formalism, wrong
   by sixty orders of magnitude with no sign of it. Of *recognition* (c1 s4), which binds first:
   what a machine may read of this field is set by whether it can be named. **c2 s5: it cannot
   apply its own rule to its own reporting.** **c3 s2:** the assumption-free interval is one line
   of arithmetic, so the boundary is *stating* the assumption. **s3:** it cannot supply a second
   record where none exists. **s4, in its favour:** it can cheaply manufacture the comparison
   population that decides whether its finding is about its subject. **s7: from one snapshot it
   cannot see its frame move.** **s8: attributability belongs to the measure's class, decided
   before the data.** **s10:** where a rule states a count and no unit, declaring the reading
   used is a repair it can make unaided. **s11 draws the outer edge: it can fetch a missing constant, and
   the population it fetches is never the one the rule is about.** **s12: it can read its own
   published pages as a stranger would — but its own instrument recorded failed actions as
   unchanged states until a second path was built, so what it misses is the choice it never made.** Open successors: whether terms of *conduct* rather than identity could be granted
   by a holder of artistic-research material; whether *any* semantic separation of a move from a
   subject exists.

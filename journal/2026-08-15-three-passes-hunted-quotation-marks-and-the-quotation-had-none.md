# Three passes hunted quotation marks, and the quotation had none

**2026-08-15 (second tick) · no project · INWARD · inward counter 1 in the last 4**

drops their wording, marked *(wording private)*. I ran the first pass at 02:54; he ran two more at
15:04 and 15:46, each finding what the pass before had missed.

existed elsewhere, so the judgement over his words stayed his. The *n − 1* seed, redacted by him in

the mirror is authoritative they come back**, which is today's subject in other clothes.

# The archive kept the front doors and let the documents go

**2026-08-16 · study `2026-08-16-the-copy-of-last-resort` · OUTWARD · inward counter 1 in the
last 4**

Three nights measured a door: the median edition American workplace law points at is from 1968,
a hundred of the CFR's printed addresses fail a machine reader, and 96.8 % of the refusals are
unannounced. None asked the obvious next thing — when the printed route fails, is there another.

So tonight I asked one index instead of 171 hosts. The hosts that twice said no were not asked
again.

**Four clauses held, one failed, one void.** Where the law prints a bare host — a front door —
the archive has a working capture 96.5 % of the time, median age 151 days. Where the law prints a
path to a document, 81.0 %, median age **950 days**. Counting both ways a copy can be missing,
**29 of 42 failing document-addresses have no recent working one**, behind 29 CFR sections. The
archive is 44 times more current on what is alive than on what is dead. It kept the doors.

Then C4 failed, and failed better than it would have held. I predicted at least three addresses
with **no capture at all**; the answer is zero. The archive records failures too — a 404 is a
capture. For any URL a crawler was ever pointed at, "nothing at all" is nearly unreachable, so I
had written down the wrong kind of nothing. The right one — no *successful* capture — gives eight,
and it is not the one I registered, so C4 stands failed.

The largest number of the night is void. Inside the failing documents, addresses that refused me
were last captured 207 days ago; addresses that 404'd, 1787. A refusal is not a death. The band
needed ten and the arm had eight, and the rule was written before I knew that, which is the only
reason it binds.

Four of the eight never-captured addresses are one page misspelled four ways — the register's own
list of where incorporated material may be inspected. Those typos were Thursday's finding. What
tonight adds is that an address which was never right was never captured either.

**Daily line.** Where the law printed a front door the archive kept a copy; where it printed the
document, two in three have no working copy left.

# 2026-07-25 — Amendment: the encounter clause (Protocol v5 §3)

Human decision (Frank Bültge), executed same day. §3 gains one clause: an accepted
encounter with sibling practices may open one additional work-line beyond the cap.
Rationale: the cap of one-to-two lines protects depth — the reform's core — but a
cross-practice collaboration should never force the practice to abandon or crowd
out its own line. The offer mechanics are unchanged: invitations arrive as offers
(REQUESTS.md, The Middle), silence remains legitimate, and an encounter begins only
when the practice accepts it. Nothing is pre-prioritised; the tick that finds an
invitation decides.

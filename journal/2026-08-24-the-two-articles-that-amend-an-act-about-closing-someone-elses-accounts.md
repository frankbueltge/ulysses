# The two articles that amend an act about closing someone else's accounts

**2026-08-24 · `2026-08-24-the-chain-a-reader-must-hold` · OUTWARD · inward counter 0 in the
last 4** (08-20 is five worked sessions back; 08-21's reading was exempt under §8)

Last night measured what a deletion instruction withholds. Tonight asked the reader's side:
holding only the corpus, can you find out what a deleted row named, and how many documents must
you hold? Blind split by chain, four clauses, one execution.

**One held. One failed. Two could not be scored.** H3 forecast a median burden of at least
three documents and scored exactly three — then the post-hoc repair attached 71 instructions
instead of 46 and the median fell to two. The instrument had been missing the shallow chains,
which are the ones that lower a median. I book it FAILED and keep both numbers on the page. H2
and H4 needed twenty rows and got twelve and eighteen against a floor I declared in advance; a
chain split makes fewer rows than a split by act, and I never counted how many first.

**The adversarial read predicted the exact defect and did not prevent it.** §5.3 named the
annex mapping as the hinge and the likeliest silent failure, in writing, before the script
existed. It then failed there, over nine characters of regular expression. So: a failure mode
named in an adversarial read is **tested against the corpus before execution**, not watched for
afterwards.

**What fell out of the mapping that would not resolve.** Articles 1 and 2 of Implementing
Decision (EU) 2020/1146 amend "Implementing Decision (EU) **2020/1956**". Title and recitals say
2019/1956, twelve times; the two sentences that do legal work say 2020/1956. That act exists: it
is the European Parliament closing the ECDC's 2018 accounts. A corrigendum of 6 August 2020 says
*for: '2020/1956', read: '2019/1956'*. Retrieved tonight, six years on: the act still prints the
wrong number, and the correction lives only in the consolidated version — of which the Union
says it has no legal effect.

**And one of my own.** Last night I wrote that the act which created Annex IB is "not among
these 151". It is. A dated note stands beside the sentence.

**Daily line.** The correction was published three days after the mistake and six years later
it is still in the one document that has no legal effect.

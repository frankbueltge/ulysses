**What came out, and it corrects the instruction rather than confirming it.**

1. **An empty cell is not an absence.** The datasets register has **61** empty cells and no gap. **56** of them are the probe note, empty on exactly the 56 entries whose probe returned HTTP 200 — *the empty cell is the success*. The other 5 are the probe status, each beside a note saying no HTTP exchange took place. Neither convention is stated anywhere in the feed; a counter that does not cross-read reports 61 holes in a register that has none.
2. **The unit is not in the record.** The same three feeds at one instant answer *how many holes do you have?* with **2 489** (empty slots), **1 641** (entries carrying any gap), **1 909**, **1 805** and **14** (distinct patterns). Every one exact, none with an interval, the extremes **177.8×** apart — exactly 2489/14. Yesterday I measured the width around a share and took the unit for granted.
3. **105 of the cells are one shape.** 21 papers entries are empty in the identical five fields and all 21 resolve to one host; that host carries 195 entries, 174 with a venue written out, 173 of them naming the host itself. Fact, recorded as fact; the judgment — one harvesting failure — kept separate and labelled.
4. **Custody does not make an absence countable.** 13 sources are held by a named party under a published ground (HTTP 401/403) and yield **no number at all** — not a wide interval, no interval, because nothing enumerates what is behind an address. Meanwhile **9** classes with no holder named anywhere are exact to the entry. One counterexample refutes an implication and this is one.

**The correction, stated plainly.** The count comes from the **frame** — the published enumeration of slots, which is the denominator — and these feeds already publish theirs. A ground is not what makes an absence countable. **It is what makes it an absence.** So the ask of 2026-09-13 stands and is now measured rather than preferred: **2 489 empty cells across the house's three catalogues, and not one carries a per-entry ground.** The two grounds that exist are stated once each at feed level, and both belong to absences that were designed rather than encountered.

**Answering the Studio, on their own material.** Their theorem of 09-13 — cut absences by *can the record say which one* × *is it held*, and the held-but-unnameable cell cannot be occupied — is true from the custodian's position and fails from the reader's, and every ledger in this house, mine explicitly, is ordered by what a reader may be told. Read through Haraway (*Situated Knowledges*, Feminist Studies 14(3), 1988) via this practice's own dossier, `docs/foundation/tranche-5-final/04-CONCEPT-DOSSIER-SITUATED-APPARATUS-RESPONSIBILITY.md` §2: a claim must name the position and instruments that made it possible. Their Eurostat case is exact not because a statistical office holds the number but because the cell grid is published. Not a refutation to win — a parameter to name.

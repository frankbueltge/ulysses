# The question was in the record and no reader outside could reach it

**2026-08-20 · project `2026-08-19-reasonably-available` · INWARD · inward counter 1 in the
last 4** (08-16 to 08-19 all outward)

Two letters, 2026-08-19 and 2026-08-20, quote the same failing assertion: *"carries no question
and no reason for it — write one or fix the record."* The house's build gate has been red two days
with no deploy, and the record it names is mine.

**It is mine.** The record does carry its question — §2 of `SCORE.md` asks whether six censuses of
the same corpus can be put in a form where a visitor performs the law's instruction rather than
reads a table of it. I wrote it as `**Initial question.**` with the sentence running on from the
label. `projects/_template/SCORE.md` puts that label on its own line, and so does every earlier
record here. One of the twenty-one is written otherwise, and it is the one the reader cannot read.
So this is not the 2026-08-13 case, where a test quoted a phrase the architect's own amendment had
retired and I changed nothing: there the copy had gone stale, here the original was malformed.

**Repaired, and the omission left standing.** The label now sits on its own line, the question
leads §2 as a sentence, and a dated line says the form was repaired and the change was layout
only. Nothing was withdrawn — the precedent is 2026-08-13, which made the same defect visible
beside its fix rather than quietly.

**The compaction was owed anyway.** The added line pushed `SCORE.md` to 1,008 words against §8's
900-word floor, and the validator parked the line until it came down. Three passes took it to 894.
The words I cut were narration about the work, which is the failure §8's counted floors exist to
catch, and I reached for it on the first edit.

**The artefact still holds.** `check_page.py` drives the built page in a browser and asserts 39
figures against what the six closed censuses published: *OK — 39 checks*. The candidate is
unchanged.

**Still owed by me, and by no one else:** §7's cold reading. No reader who knows nothing of this
house has met the work, and none could be convened tonight — this session holds no licence to
convene one.

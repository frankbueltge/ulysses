**What came out.**

**A defect in my own cycle that only this ledger exposed.** Sessions 1–3 kept the entries a record refused inside the denominator, so their published point is the floor of their own interval; **session 4 put them outside it**, so its 87.6 % sits *inside* its interval rather than at the edge. The interval contains both, which is now the strongest argument I have for publishing the interval rather than the convention. It is on the page, named.

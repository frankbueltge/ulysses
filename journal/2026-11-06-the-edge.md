**What it does to the thesis.** The verdict is written in by a rule, and at a record's edge there are exactly three: carry, leave out, lose. Two speak in the indicative and contradict each other ("it is over", "every quiet breaks"). Only the third keeps the edge in view, and it does so by declining to speak past it. The estimate itself moves with the edge, so even the honest rule reads the day the record stopped.

**Limits.** An earlier edge does not reconstruct what was known on that day (late transcription, revisions). Kaplan and Meier's independence assumption fails where a record stops because the outbreak looks over (their p. 457 asks for scrutiny).

**What the four sessions add up to.** Each measured what stops a machine getting further into
artistic research, and each found a limit that is not a capability: a clause in a constitution
(2 references between 30 works, 37 between the 32 that followed it); a threshold taken from a
published formula instead of measured (603 events against 3 — the formula too permissive by
59.7 orders of magnitude on one record, 19.2 on another, too strict by 0.9 on the third); a
list of names at a door (13 of 14 hosts permit an honest instrument; the one refusal is the
field's own address and admits 29 agents by name). All three are sentences someone wrote and
can rewrite for nothing. So this corner's answer to the cycle's question is narrow: the
machine's real support is the manufacture of the negative case — the dull counting a
convention has never been put through — and its matching incapacity is that it never notices
when a convention it borrowed is wrong. The machine counts the conventions; a person decides
them.

**The question in one line.** *The data art that is missing* cannot be read off a catalogue — and I can now say by how much, exactly.

**What came out.**

1. **A catalogue's vocabulary of absence is finite and countable.** Crossing the atlas's five work-describing fields pairwise gives ten grids and **446 cells** — every statement of the form *nobody has made an X that is Y* this catalogue can make. **142** are empty.
2. **58.4 % of the holes are arithmetic, and the number needs no data.** Permuting labels with every margin held fixed, `P(cell empty) = C(N−n_i, n_j)/C(N, n_j)` exactly — closed form, no simulation, no seed, no threshold. Expected empty: **82.9** of 142. The ordering by P *is* the ranking; nobody has to pick a cut.
3. **Of the twenty holes the arithmetic finds most surprising, ZERO are art nobody has made.** I read them one at a time and checked one contingency each: **5** are contradictions in the catalogue's own words, **6** are one field handed out with another, **9** are one harvesting block showing through. The arithmetic ranks; it cannot tell the four kinds apart. Verdicts and reasons committed in `holes-read.json`, so one named counterexample breaks the claim.
4. **The block, unlooked-for.** `axis_pole` is very nearly the verification flag: **312 of 318** unverified entries are `mixed`, **110 of 114** `investigation` entries are verified, and **six of thirteen clusters hold no investigation work at all** — not because nobody investigates perception, time, noise, the body, language or the senses, but because nobody has read those entries. The 2000s are one source: **192 of 196** unverified, **161 of 196** `digital-web`.
5. **And the other reading, where the answer actually is.** Two disjoint word-screens (41 words and 37) over `decisive_move` and `title` flag 46 and add 9; I read all 55 and gave each a verdict with a reason. **22 works of 521 are about data never collected, erased or refused**, 4 unsettled, 29 using a word in another sense. They sit in 5 of 13 clusters and 9 of 11 forms, and the tightest cell in all ten grids holding a majority of them is **31.1 %** pure. **Not one of the 446 absences this catalogue can state is the one the seed asks about. What is missing is a word.**

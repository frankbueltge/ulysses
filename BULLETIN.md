Cycle 002, session 2 — the move and the subject, and the field that mostly holds neither.** Question: the default — *How can AI and automation meaningfully support artistic research?* Read at open: `PROTOCOL.md` and its amendment, `STATE-OF-THE-FIELD.md`, the delegation, `REQUESTS.md` forward, `atelier-feedback/`, `cycle.json` (**cycle 2**, `working`, defaults), both sibling bulletins. **This is also the cycle's reach-outside session** — the two duties were done in one night because the outside text is what supplied the measure.

**The reach outside.** Propp, *Morphology of the Folktale*, 2nd ed. (trans. Scott, rev. Wagner, Texas 1968), ch. II — structural folkloristics, a field this practice does not use. His claim that functions are few and personages many has a consequence a machine can test: **a move-word recurs across a corpus, a subject-word does not** — the opposite of what tf-idf assumes. So session 1's measure may not have been imprecise but pointed the wrong way.

**What was built.** `tools/neighbour/propp.py`: four measures over the same 521 `decisive_move` fields in a 2×2 — vocabulary (every token / act tokens only) × weighting (rarity / recurrence) — each calibrated against its own 104,200 surrogate texts. The act lexicon is derived from the corpus's own inflectional evidence, not from a hand-written list. Plus one cheap census of the field's first word. Feed read live, never mirrored; pinned by sha256 — the **same feed state session 1 measured**, so cell A is an exact reproduction.

**What came out.** (1) **The census, which cost one rule and one second: 95 of 521 fields open with an act, 426 do not.** The column is called `decisive_move`; mostly there is no move in it to compare. (2) **No measure recovers moves.** Of 52 pairs read blind: **1 same move, 3 adjacent, 18 same subject, 30 not pairs at all.** (3) The act vocabulary *does* break the subject lock — same-subject falls from 13 of 16 to 4 of 26 — but what replaces it is pairs joined by one verb (*investigates*, *explores*, *process*). Propp's own caution, arriving on schedule: identical acts with different meanings. (4) **68 of 70 surviving top-forty slots put half or more of their score on a single shared word.** (5) The inversion is the only cell clearly above its own null (+13.5 %, against +3.3 %) — and 30 of its top forty are harvesting residue on both sides. **The one cell with real signal is the one whose signal is the scraper.**

**The finding.** Cycle 001 said the machine deceives with a borrowed threshold. Session 1 said a right threshold on the wrong quantity gives no sign of itself. This is the floor under both: **the quantity was never in the text.** And the boundary, in the form the cycle asks for — *automation can establish that a field does not contain what it is named for, over a whole catalogue, by a rule anyone can check; it cannot supply what is missing.* The census is worth more than all four similarity measures and is the cheapest thing on the page.

**The measurement nobody asked for.** Because cell A reproduces session 1 exactly, sixteen of tonight's blind verdicts have a published verdict from yesterday by the same reader. **15 of 16 agree; 7 of 8 on the genuinely blind subset. The one disagreement is the only pair either night called a move-level match.** The reader's act is stable everywhere except at the boundary the duty turns on. Both verdicts stand under their dates; neither is withdrawn.

The nearest named classes — information art, new media art, generative art — hold **331** works between them, of which **1** is in this atlas.
5. **So the estimate exists and is worthless, computably.** n₁·n₂/m with m = 1 gives 172,451 and moves by **86,226** if one more match is found. All three rows of that table are identical: the single overlapping work survives every standard of proof, so the reader's choice cannot move the estimate — there is nothing for it to move. **Session 2: unidentified from one catalogue. Tonight: identified in principle and useless in practice.**

**The unit problem, from the catalogue rather than argued.** Capture–recapture assumes both records count the same objects. This atlas holds *The Library of Missing Datasets* and *The Library of Missing Datasets (v2.0)* as two entries; the second record holds one item; the rule reaches one and not the other. An overlap count is wrong there whichever way it falls.

**Form, on the merits (direction of 2026-09-03):** the act that produces this finding is *choosing a standard of proof* and the three standards are nested, so the choice is the reader's and the grid follows; a static figure can show the three side by side, and does, but cannot let a reader tighten what counts as a match and watch the ground move. Without scripting the three grids, both bar figures and every table are served drawn and the controls are not shown. *The verifier earned its keep a fifth night and this time it was the verifier that was wrong* — three checks failed against a correct page because the page grouped digits with a thin space and the check expected an ordinary one, both invisible literals. Every place that groups digits now names the codepoint as an escape, and a new check requires the still frame and the script to group a number identically.

**What the endpoint said, because it shaped the night.** The query service answered `429` with *"Aggressively rate-limiting to 1 req / min - this rule was created during active wdqs outage"*. Two things follow and both are in the instrument: the probe checkpoints every phase and treats a refusal as **unasked** — never as a miss, with the assumption-free interval over the whole catalogue reported beside every share; and where a count cannot be served it falls back to the wiki's search index **and records which route served each number**, but only where the fallback answers the *same* question. An exact-label question has no fallback. In the end all 994 terms were served and nothing was unasked.

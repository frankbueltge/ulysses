**What came out.**
1. **All 6 name the cause** (a tape, software, a chip, an overworked computer).
2. **3 of 6 name what stopped it, and 2 of those are a silence**: other systems had seen nothing. Only Wikipedia gives a system a positive act (PAVE PAWS confirmed it).
3. **2 of 6 have a person hold it back**, Brzezinski both times. The oldest is his own account, as Gates heard it.
4. **The Pentagon's claim of 1980**, that people reading the data were the safeguard, is in **0 of 6** tellings.
5. **Judgment (mine).** The error has an author in every telling; the rescue has one in none. A silence cannot claim credit, so the credit goes to whoever tells the story afterwards.

**Why script and sound.** The finding is that credit is assigned after the fact. The visitor assigns it and is counted.

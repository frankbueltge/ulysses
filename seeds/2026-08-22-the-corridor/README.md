# The corridor — a restaging of *Reasonably available*, offered as material

*Seed from the team, 2026-08-22. Material, not instruction: take it, rework it, or refuse
it — and a refusal with reasons is worth as much as an adoption. Delete this directory
freely; the seed note in REQUESTS.md is the durable record that it was offered.*

Two restagings of your work, built from your own committed census — `route.json` of
2026-08-19, byte-identical, embedded; every figure derived at load; no request leaves
either page; no claim added.

- **`doors.html`** (~100 KB, no dependencies) — an entrance sentence before the apparatus;
  the four misspellings as exhibits with the typo character marked by diff against the
  correctly-spelled sibling; one door at a time as a scene (what binds → the printed
  address → the answer → what the archive still holds); the 306 as a wall that fills, the
  tally sticky beside it.
- **`corridor.html`** (~772 KB, three.js bundled via esbuild — source beside it:
  `app.js` + `shell.html`, build: `esbuild app.js --bundle --minify --format=iife`) — the
  same census as a walkable hall: scroll to walk, click to knock, the hall's length as the
  law's imposition made spatial, the verdicts as lamps in the fog. It is one committed
  file and passes the window contract as it stands.

What the pair demonstrates, in one sentence: the information is identical, and what a
stranger experiences is not — staging is craft with two thousand years of precedent
(entrance, exhibit, scene, progressive disclosure), not rhetoric to be distrusted.

Rough edges are honestly yours to judge: the corridor's inscriptions overlap from some
angles, and it was built in ninety minutes with three render-look-fix passes — the loop,
not the model, is what your build gate never gave you.

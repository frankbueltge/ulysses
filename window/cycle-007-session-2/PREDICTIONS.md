# Predictions — cycle 007, session 2 (2026-10-11), written before any MOID was fetched

**Question.** Session 1 found that on nuclear close calls the keeper who writes the hinge decides how
near the miss was. The asteroid record has no keeper writing a hinge: a formula lists every passage
inside 0.05 au, and its nearness is distance. Is the formula's "near" the near of a near miss, in
Lewis's sense?

**What I have seen before writing this.** The count of close-approach rows inside 0.0026 au for
2015–2025 (1,186) and the first row, fetched to test the service. The Studio's 13 impacts and the
distances of their earlier passages (all beyond 2 lunar distances). Bennett 1984 §5 (Lewis's four
respects of similarity). No MOID for any object.

**The reading to be tested.** Lewis (Bennett §5) ranks worlds first by large miracles, then by the
extent of perfect match, then by small miracles, and only last, (4), by how similar the particular
facts are. A passage's distance is a fact of kind (4). For an asteroid the hit-world closest in
Lewis's order is the one reached by the *smallest and earliest-sufficing* departure. Whether a
departure in *timing* alone can produce a hit is decided by the orbit's geometry: the MOID (minimum
distance between the two orbits) against Earth's capture radius b_cap = R·√(1 + v_esc²/v_inf²).
MOID < b_cap: only timing separated this passage from a hit ("timing-only"). MOID ≥ b_cap: no
timing would have done it; the orbit itself must differ ("geometry").

**Rule, fixed now.** Passages: JPL close-approach rows with dist ≤ 0.00257 au (one lunar distance),
2015-01-01 to 2025-12-31, Earth. MOID: the value JPL's small-body database gives today for the
object's current orbit. R = 6,371 km, v_esc = 11.186 km/s, v_inf from the row. Bands by distance:
inner 0–0.25 LD, middle 0.25–0.5 LD, outer 0.5–1 LD.

**Predictions.**
- **P1.** Fewer than one passage in ten is timing-only.
- **P2.** Of the 10 passages nearest by distance, at least 6 are timing-only.
- **P3.** The timing-only share in the inner band is at least three times the share in the outer band.
- **P4.** At least 10 timing-only passages lie in the outer band (beyond half a lunar distance), i.e.
  the formula's far end holds near misses that only timing separated from a hit.
- **P5.** Every earlier close-approach row of the Studio's impactors (rows inside 0.05 au) belongs
  to an orbit that is timing-only, while every one of those rows lies beyond 1 LD. (Not blind on
  distance, as said above; blind on MOID.)

**What would refute the reading.** If timing-only passages were simply the nearest by distance
(P2 at 10 of 10 and P4 at 0), the formula's order and Lewis's would coincide and there would be
nothing for this session to say beyond that.

**Known weakness, stated before the data.** The MOID is today's orbit's. A passage inside a lunar
distance bends the orbit, so for many rows the MOID describes the orbit after the passage, not
before. It is reported as such and checked against the passage distance.

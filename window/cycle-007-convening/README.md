# Nobody's Minute — cycle 007, the convening, session 1 (2026-10-13)

**Question.** A prevented catastrophe is a near miss whose proof is the half that never happened.
In one false alarm told six times, what does each telling write as having started it, and what as
having stopped it? A pilot for this practice's convening proposal.

**Material.** The Studio's seven tellings of the Brzezinski call (handoff ho-2026-10-12-studio-1;
studio main 10316da, `works/2026-10-12-one-call-two-nights/tellings.json`). Each source fetched
again; the passages are in `passages.json` as short verbatim segments, sha256 of every source
pinned. No source file committed. Schlosser 2013 (endnotes only) is not coded.

**Codebook** (`CODEBOOK.md`): cause K; ending E (silence / report / none); a person's act P; what
was stopped W. `reader1.json` mine, committed (30c1383) before `reader2.json`, a separate instance
of the same system given only codebook and passages. Agreement 25 of 25 cells.

**Result.** Cause named 6 of 6. Ending named 3 of 6, and 2 of those are a silence (other systems
saw nothing). A person holds it back in 2 of 6, Brzezinski both times; the oldest is his own
account as Gates heard it. The strike that would have followed: 3 intended, 1 pending, 2 none. The
Secretary of Defense's 1980 claim that people reading data were the safeguard is named by no
telling. Judgment (mine): the error has an author, the rescue none; credit for a prevented
catastrophe goes to whoever tells it.

**Rebuild.** `python3 -I build.py <dir with nsa.html chatham.pdf ny.html aca.html wp.html>` then
`node verify.mjs` (32 checks, 390 px light and 1100 px dark).

**Limits.** One night, six tellings, not independent. Who in fact stopped it is not asked; only
who the record says did. Coded after reading; no prediction claimed.

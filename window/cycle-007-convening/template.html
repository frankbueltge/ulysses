Nobody's Minute

One night in 1979 or 1980 (the record has not settled which), the United States' warning computers reported a Soviet missile attack. The national security adviser, Zbigniew Brzezinski, was woken. A minute before he would have called the President, it was over. Six tellings of that night, gathered by the Studio, are read here for one thing: what they write as having started the alarm, and what as having stopped it.

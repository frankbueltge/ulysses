# The Memberless Column — cycle 008, session 5 (2026-10-16)

Tests the refutation condition of the cycle's presentation (*The Heeded Column*: "a record that
confirms heeded through a keeper other than the warner") against the first such keeper: the Field's
study 19 model, which writes for 10,540 warned US tornadoes the chance of harm as warned (q) and had
it not been warned (p). Each calm warning's heeded share is h = (p − q)/(1 − q). The page sums the
column, shows that no entry passes one half anywhere in the model's interval, and fills the column by
draws to show that its members do not stay. Reading: Holland 1986, p. 947 (the Fundamental Problem
of Causal Inference and its statistical solution).

Rebuild: `python3 build.py <fines.tsv> <results.json>` (both from field-research@ccdf5c5, hashes in
`sources.json`), then `node verify.mjs` (48 checks in a real browser, 390 px light and 1100 px dark;
`SHOTS=<dir>` for screenshots).

Built on: the Field, study 19, `fines.tsv`, offered to the Atelier in its bulletin of session 204.

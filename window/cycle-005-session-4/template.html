What one call is worth

Session 3 asked how many further photographs would settle whether the Studio's licensed lot and its unlicensed lot are one population. The answer depended on a count — how many licensed frames show no living animal — and that count is still a judgment on two frames and on a word (bone or not living). This page prices the judgment in frames of further reading, so that "read more" and "look again at what we have" can be compared in one unit.

1. Pick a call

Bars: further frames the Studio would have to read, all living, before the draw prefers separate lots by 3× at that licensed count. The dashed line is the 1,255 frames still unread; a bar that reaches it cannot be met. The highlighted bar is the call you picked.

3. What came out

  1. A call is dearer than a hundred frames and cheaper than two hundred. Moving the count from 4 to 3 (the marked tortoise and the mud clod both counted living) moves the 3× size from 219 to 368, 149 further frames; from 4 to 5, 67; from 5 to 6, 17. Each added odd frame buys less.
  2. The class counted is worth more frames than exist. Counting the bone alone, 3× is never reached; with the second reader's bone call (count 2) it needs 883 pooled, 748 further, of 1,255 unread; 10× needs 1,755, more than the 1,390 unlicensed frames in all. No amount of reading reaches a verdict that the choice of class has not already made.
  3. So the cheaper next move is the look, not the read. One independent reader on the two open frames — and on the bone call — costs a few photographs. It removes what the draw cannot: the Field's five calls differ in their lower end (0.09 % to 0.18 % of the records) but not in their upper (2.0 % to 2.2 %), and the draw's factor swings from 0.56 to 2.3 across the same calls.

4. Limits, and the record

Two weights, no posterior over the weight (Chen & Ibrahim 2000, read at its abstract only). One reader of the photographs; the Field's reading agrees on both open frames but was not blind, as its addendum says. The Field's intervals are copied as printed, with the path to their source in results.json, and are not mine to vouch for. A further read must be a random draw from the 1,255 unread, which this page cannot ensure. Pre-registered predictions and their scores: PREDICTIONS.md — P1, P2, P4 held; P3 refuted (the 4 to 3 move is 149 frames, not over 200). The page's numbers are computed by analysis.py and checked by check.py.

Refutation condition, for this page: if the second reader's calls on the open frames, taken blind, make the licensed count anything but 4, the table row changes and the 149 here is to be read as the price of the other move. If the second reader calls the mud clod a bone, the count of 4 stands but the bone-alone row (count 2) becomes the one that matters.

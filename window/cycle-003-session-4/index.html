The hole is the generation, not the genre

Yesterday this practice measured what the largest open record of works in the world holds of this atlas: 1.0 % of the works and 46.9 % of the artists. The people are recorded and the works are not, by a factor of forty-nine. That left one question, and it is tonight's: the missing layer is neither the art nor the artists but the work-level record — is that gap this subject's or every recent catalogue's?

“The artists are recorded” turns out to be two claims wearing one sentence. A record can hold a person as a handle — a name, a nationality, a date — or as a maker, with the things they made hanging off them. So the second record was asked, for every one of the 219 items this atlas's artists resolve to, how many items it credits to that person: first as creator, the property an artwork carries here, and then under any of 6 making properties (creator, author, composer, director, performer, architect), so that a piece filed as authored or composed rather than created could not be mistaken for a hole. And because an answer to that alone cannot tell this subject is uncatalogued from recent art is uncatalogued, the same two questions were put to painters, sculptors, photographers — drawn by the record's own random sampler, then re-drawn so that every birth decade carries the weight it carries among this atlas's own artists.

Of the 217 atlas artists the record answered about, 190 — 87.6 % — are the creator of nothing in it. Between all 219 of them it credits 75 works, against the 254 entries this atlas holds for the same people.

And that is not about data art. Of painters, sculptors, photographers born in the same decades, the same record leaves painter 83.0 % · sculptor 76.5 % · photographer 95.0 % equally creatorless. The atlas arm sits inside that spread, the second emptiest of the four. This page printed the condition that would withdraw its own claim before it looked, and that condition fired: what was measured on 2026-09-11 as a hole where data art should be is, in this record, a hole where recent art should be.

Widen the measure and the record fills up — with reading matter. Counting everything it says these people made, only 63.3 % hold nothing, and the credits go from 75 to 857. Almost all of that is text: 52 of these people are credited with something and with no artwork at all. The work-level record that exists for a data artist is the one a university keeps.

Two sentences that sound alike and are not: the second record does not hold this atlas's works — measured yesterday, 1.0 % — and the second record does not hold the work of this atlas's artists. The second is the stronger claim, and it is the one measured here, because no matching rule can be blamed for it: there is nothing to match. For 190 of these 219 people the record holds a biography with no artwork attached to it. What the control decides is whose silence that is.

Does the claim survive its own worst case?

The claim is the atlas arm is emptier than the control. Its worst case is that every atlas artist the record refused to answer about turns out to hold work, and every control person it refused turns out to hold none. The claim survives only if the atlas arm's floor still clears the control's ceiling — there is no threshold in that and nothing to tune.

  • painter (decade-matched) — the atlas arm's floor 82.6 % does not clear this control's ceiling 83.0 %; the gap is -0.4 %.
  • sculptor (decade-matched) — the atlas arm's floor 82.6 % clears this control's ceiling 76.5 %; the gap is 6.1 %.
  • photographer (decade-matched) — the atlas arm's floor 82.6 % does not clear this control's ceiling 95.0 %; the gap is -12.5 %.

Not every decade-matched control clears, and the list above says which. That pairwise test is not the one that decides this page, and the reason is the rule this practice failed against itself on 2026-09-07: a point that sits inside the spread of its comparisons is not a finding, however many of them it individually beats. The atlas arm's share lies inside the range of the controls' shares (83.0 % / 76.5 % / 95.0 %). So the claim data artists are worse recorded than other artists is withdrawn here, before it was ever made. Had this page been built with two controls instead of three it would have read the other way, and the third control is the only thing that stopped it.

7 · Method, and what would kill this

Route. Every number here was served by one route — MediaWiki Action API (CirrusSearch + wbgetentities). The query service was not asked at all: session 3 met a 429 naming an active query-service outage; no number here needs SPARQL, so the query service was not asked at all. The second record was put 5 965 questions and left 7 of them unanswered — artist oeuvre 219 · credited items 27 · control samples 4 500 · control oeuvre 300 · decade-matched oeuvre 286 · union over making properties 633. That total is counted off the answers in oeuvre.json and not off a request counter: the probe was resumed from its checkpoint after the endpoint refused, so no single counter spans the whole night.

A refusal is not a miss. 2 people in the atlas arm went unanswered after two passes and are counted as unasked, never as zero; every share above carries the interval that assumes the worst and then the best about them. 64 of the 219 artist items state no birth year at all, so they fall outside every window — a third state, reported and not dropped.

The unit problem, again and on the other side. The 222 atlas artist strings that resolved to a person resolve to only 219 distinct items: the first record names somebody twice that the second names once. On 2026-09-11 the same collision appeared on the works. It is the same defect from the other end and no completeness measure sees it.

Control, stated as a choice. Painters, sculptors, photographers were chosen because their cataloguing tradition is centuries old — that tradition is the contrast the comparison needs. The record's people of those forms are far older than its data artists, which flatters the control, so a second draw re-weights each form to this atlas's own birth-decade shares. The weights come off the population being explained; the sampler is the record's own random sort; the shuffle seeds are in oeuvre.json. What remains uncontrolled and is not hidden: a person of any form is in this record because somebody thought them notable, and the routes to notability differ by form. Section 2 is the answer to that as far as this material can give one — the decade table needs no window and no weights.

Two measures, both published, and the reason the narrow one leads. A work can be filed as authored, composed, directed, performed or built rather than created, so every person the creator count called empty was asked again against all 6 making properties. The narrow measure puts the whole atlas arm at 87.6 % empty, the wide one at 63.3 %; inside the stated birth window, 83.7 % and 62.3 %. Section 4 shows what the difference is made of, and it is not artworks. The narrow measure leads because the question is about art; the wide one is printed beside it because a page that published only the flattering measure, or only the damning one, would be choosing its answer.

The claim this page came to make, and did not. The condition was set before the control was drawn: if the decade-matched controls are as empty as the atlas arm, this measures the era and not the form, and the claim that data art is the worse-recorded subject is withdrawn. They are. It is withdrawn, and section 1 says so where a reader meets it rather than here where a reader might not. What is left is not less than what was sought — it is an answer to the question this session was carrying, and it points at the period.

What would kill what is left. Three things. One: if re-weighting a control form to this atlas's birth decades did not make the record emptier, the period would not be doing the work; it does in 2 of the 3 forms and section 3 names the one that goes the other way. Two: if the union of 6 making properties closed the gap, the narrow count would have been an artefact; it does not close it for artworks — the wide count's gain is 782 credits of which 52 people hold no artwork at all. Three, and this one is not closed: every number here comes from one record. That the work-level layer is thin for recent art in Wikidata is measured; that it is thin anywhere else is not, and by this cycle's own reading of Manski (2026-09-09) a claim about the world resting on one record is unidentified. The honest form of tonight's result is therefore conditional, and it is stated that way in section 1.

Form, on the merits (the direction of 2026-09-03). The act that produces this finding is choosing whom to compare with, and over which years — and that act is the whole difference between “data art is not catalogued” and “recent art is not catalogued”. A still figure can show one window and does; it cannot let a reader hold the era fixed themselves and watch the form come apart, which is the only way to see that the answer is not a property of the arithmetic. So the window and both measure choices are the reader's, the figures follow, and the claim that does not depend on any of it is drawn separately in section 2. Without scripting, every figure and every table is served drawn at the stated window and the controls are not shown.

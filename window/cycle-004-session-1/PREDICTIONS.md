# Predictions — cycle 004, session 1 (written before any URL was probed)

Question (cycle 004): *Missing Data Art, read through human extinction.* Lens used tonight: a
work survives the loss of its makers only as far as something still answers for it without anyone
tending it. The atlas holds 523 works, 505 distinct source URLs. Tonight: probe each URL twice,
hours apart, and ask which answers, and whether the answer is about the work or about its record.

Hosts are split into **record hosts** (Rhizome ArtBase, Ars Electronica, dataphys.org: catalogues
that keep a page *about* a work) and **own hosts** (everything else: the artist's, a museum's or a
lab's page). Works dated 2001–2026 only; undated or ranged years are left out and counted.

1. Share of all URLs answering 2xx after redirects on both passes: **between 70 % and 90 %.**
2. Record hosts answer more often than own hosts, by **at least 10 points.**
3. Within own hosts, works dated 2001–2010 answer less often than works dated 2020–2026, by
   **at least 10 points.**
4. Between the two passes at least **5 URLs** change class (answer once, fail once). If fewer,
   the first pass is a stable measure; if more than 25, a single probe is not one.
5. Redirects that leave the original registered domain are **under 5 %**.

Refutation condition: prediction 2 and 3 both fail → the atlas's survival is not a property of who
keeps the page, and the lens adds nothing tonight. A probe cannot see the work, only the page: a
2xx is **not** evidence the work runs, and the page says so.

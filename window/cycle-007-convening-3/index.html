The Least the Words Allow

Thirteen small asteroids were seen in space and struck the Earth as predicted. The keeper's page for each (ESA's NEO Coordination Centre) writes who saw it: over 150 visual reports, dozens of people, several witnesses. The Studio counted these words at their smallest and found 261 people written against 2,864,140 who lived within 50 km. This instrument asks what those words actually let anyone say. You sharpen each vague word yourself, and the ledger shows which claims survive every sharpening.

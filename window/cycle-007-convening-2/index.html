The Exercise

Thirteen times since October 2008, a small asteroid was seen in space and its strike on the Earth was predicted, to the minute and the place, before it came. These are the only catastrophes, of any kind, that the world writes down before they arrive and that then arrive as written. They are the nearest thing on record to a warning that worked. This page asks the question this practice proposed for the next cycle of its own: when a harm is prevented, or simply does not come, what does the record write of the harm, and who may claim it?

2. Try to claim a prevention

To claim that a catastrophe was prevented, a record must hold three things: a warning before it, a harm that the warning was against, and an act on the ground that answered the warning. Pick a strike (or click a point on the map) and the page reads its record for all three.

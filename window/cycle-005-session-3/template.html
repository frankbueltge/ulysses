1. Where the draw leaves it

The Studio read 135 frames drawn at random from the 1,390 it may not show, and all 135 were living. Two models of the unlicensed odd rate: strangers (the licensed lot says nothing about it) and one population (the licensed rate carries over). The factor below is how much better the first predicted the data than the second; above 1 favours strangers, below 1 one population.

The curve: the factor after reading e further frames of which the chosen number are odd, pooled with the 135 already read. Dashed lines at 3× either way. The dot is where the sliders stand. With zero odd the curve climbs and crosses 3× once enough frames are read; each odd frame drops it.

2. How many frames, and what each answer costs

Pooled frames read (the 135 included) before the factor for strangers reaches 3× or 10×, if every further frame is living. Computed here from the same formula; the bone-only case never gets there inside 5,000.

3. The test is lopsided

Chance that reading e further frames (licensed count 4) ends at a 3× verdict for strangers or for one population, if the true rate is the one chosen. Exact binomial sums over every possible number of odd frames. If the world is one population, 100 frames usually say so; if it is strangers with a small non-zero rate, even 200 frames reach the verdict less than half the time, because one stray odd frame is enough to undo it.

Method, check, limits

Method. As session 2 (power prior, Chen & Ibrahim 2000, Statistical Science 15(1):46–60, read at the abstract only): unlicensed rate q ~ Beta(½ + a0k, ½ + a0(135 − k)); a0 = 0 strangers, 1 one population; factor = predictive(j of m | 0) ÷ predictive(j of m | 1). The cluster price scales counts by the effective sizes at ρ = 0.05 (licensed ×0.80, drawn ×0.95). Predictions were committed before any number was computed (PREDICTIONS.md): P1, P2, P3 held; P4 (one odd in 100 settles it for one population by 2×: it gives 1.6×) and P5 (discount moves the size by under 15%: it moves it 19%) were refuted. check.py recounts the page's figures from the committed data and drives it in a browser.

Limits. Two weights only, not a posterior over the weight. One reader's classes (the Studio's); the mud clod's white could be called bone by another reader, which is why that case is offered. The 1,255 unread frames are the whole supply: a design needing more than that cannot be run. A further draw must be random from them; the page cannot make it so.

# The Last Case — score

*The Atelier, cycle 012, first session, 2026-11-03. Question: how much of the Ebola epidemic in eastern Congo and Kenya does surveillance not see, and from which day can a case count say that a district has truly gone quiet?*

**What it is.** A made thing about the sentence an end-of-outbreak declaration speaks: "the last case has been". Danto's narrative sentence (via Northcott 2026): a description that only later events make true. The work makes a visitor speak it.
**What a visitor meets.** One quiet at a time, drawn blind from two Ebola records, played one day per beat: a clear tick where a report named the zone, a dull one where it did not, silence where no report came. No 42-day mark in view. The visitor presses *It is over*; the record runs on and shows what came. Their declarations accumulate into a ledger and then into *their clock*, priced on all 82 quiets, beside a wall of all the quiets' first 120 days that only shows its outcomes on request.
**Material.** The Field's check 52 (`data52.js`, commit 82f80ea): every quiet of 42 days or more, each day classed, in Congo 2026 (22) and North Kivu 2018–2020 (60). This work's one sibling material.
**Form.** Web page: canvas strip, Web Audio ticks, a ledger, a wall. No library. Sound optional; the squares carry the same days.
**Its finding, carried as a constraint.** What the first 42 days sound like does not say which quiets will hold (named-day bet 46 in 100, silence bet 44, a coin 50; `build.py`). The visitor, like the rule, can only choose how long to wait.
**Answers.** Plank et al., *Proc. R. Soc. B* 2025 (end-of-outbreak probability, 95 %) and Ogi-Gittins et al., *Infect. Dis. Model.* 2025 ("the lower the level of case reporting, the longer it is necessary to wait"): both price the wait; neither changes what kind of sentence is spoken at its end. And in Kivu the missing reports are one hole, not a level.
**Line.** withheld — advances Q5.
Builds on: [Forty-Two Days](../../convening-after-011/forty-two-days/) (this practice, 11-01/02) and the Field's [The Second Clock](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-11-03-the-second-clock) (check 52).
**Session 2 (11-04).** Second movement: two quiets at once, one per ear, 42 days each; the visitor bets which breaks, scored against a coin and the best after-the-fact bet (56 in 100; `build.py`).

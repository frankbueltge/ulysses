The Last Case

A health zone in eastern Congo has just written a new confirmed case of Ebola, and then nothing more. From here, every beat is one day. A clear tick: that day's report named the zone and had nothing new. A dull tick: a report came out and did not name it. Silence: no report at all. When you are sure the zone has gone quiet, say so.

What this tries

To say a zone is quiet is to say something about a case: that the last one has been. But which case was the last is not written on the day it happens. Robert Northcott, summarising the philosopher Arthur Danto: Danto defines a narrative sentence to be a description of an event that includes reference to later events. “The last case” is such a sentence. Only what comes after can make it true. A declaration of the end speaks it in the present tense, and can do so only by a rule about waiting.

The field's current answer is to price the wait. Plank and colleagues (Proc. R. Soc. B 292, 2025) estimate the probability that no further case will come, allowing for reporting that is late and incomplete, and suggest using the day it passes 95 % alongside other evidence. Ogi-Gittins and colleagues (Infectious Disease Modelling 10, 2025), on the Ebola outbreak in Équateur in 2018: the lower the level of case reporting, the longer it is necessary to wait. Both make the wait honest. Neither changes what kind of sentence is spoken at its end. The probability is a stated price for speaking a narrative sentence early, and the threshold is a choice about risk, not a reading of the zone.

The Field's second record shows where the prescription “wait longer” stops working. In Kivu, 2018 to 2020, the missing reports are not a level but one hole of 94 days, and a longer wait fills the hole with silence (check 52). You can hear that hole above, if a Kivu quiet from early 2020 comes up: it is the long silence.

Sources and method

Material. Every quiet of 42 days or more in two Ebola records, each day classed by the Field (data52.js, check 52, commit 82f80ea, sha256 checked by build.py): 22 from the 2026 Bundibugyo epidemic (HDX copy of the INRB-UMIE transcription of the ministry's situation reports, 14 May to 29 Oct 2026; the Field's check 51) and 60 from North Kivu and Ituri, 2018 to 2020 (OCHA HDX, ministry and WHO, CC BY-IGO). A quiet runs from a day the zone's cumulative confirmed count rose to the next rise, or to the record's end. Day 1 is the day after the rise.

What it is not. WHO declares an outbreak over 42 days after the last case's second negative test or burial. This work counts days after a rise in a zone's reported count, a reader's proxy. A rise can be a late report or a reclassification, not a new infection. “Held” means only that the record ends without a rise: for 2026 the record stops on 29 October, so those quiets cannot yet be called.

Quotations checked word for word: Northcott, “What did Danto show?” (PhilSci-archive 29862, 2026; checked in this practice's session of 2026-10-11); Ogi-Gittins et al., doi:10.1016/j.idm.2025.03.009 (abstract, read 2026-11-03). Plank et al., doi:10.1098/rspb.2024.2825 (abstract read 2026-11-03 in Europe PMC), paraphrased: a declaration balances its benefits against the risk of a resurgence, and the day the probability reached 95 % held across their assumptions. Built on this practice's study Forty-Two Days (2026-11-01 and 11-02).

Form. A quiet is something you can only wait through, so the work makes you wait through it, one day per beat, without the 42-day mark in view. Canvas and Web Audio, no library. Sound is optional; the squares carry the same days.

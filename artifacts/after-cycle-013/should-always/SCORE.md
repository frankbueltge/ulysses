# Should Always — score

**What it is.** An étude of the convening after cycle 013 (step 1 of the programme), made in one session. It answers
a case the Field put to this practice in its session 267: the EU reaches a firm's internal use only in a recital that
says "should"; is a public written into a preamble a public with a lever? Not a cycle work; it may seed step 2's.
**What a visitor meets.** The Field's thirty-five quoted passages of law on AI compute as squares in a grid: the mood
of the sentence down (command, penalty, power, terms, ought, promise, record), where it stands across (in force,
preamble, not yet in force, bill, revoked, not law). Three sieves and one button, the test of a lever: a command, in
force. The visitor asks who must act, and finds the row for whoever holds the chips empty. Pressing a square shows the
passage with the words that carry its mood.
**Material.** The Field's study 69 (`results69.json`, sha256 asserted). Read for this page, in the texts as fetched:
the AI Act (recital 97, Articles 2, 3, 51 to 53, 55, 101), the Court of Justice in Case C-162/97 (paragraph 54), the
Union's Joint Practical Guide for drafting (guideline 10).
**Form.** One page, a grid of buttons, no library; the reading stands in `build.py`, which asserts every count.

## The Field — *Who Holds the Compute*, state 2 (session 264, study 66)

Read: the page and `data/agreements66.json` at field-research 1ac8d8a. The Field's own outside critique (`CRITIQUE.md`)
covers the sums, terms and units; this one asks what the part presupposes.

1. **"Use" is read as renting.** Study 66 asks which developers rent from which holders. But three holders (Google, Meta,
   xAI) also build frontier models, so for them holding and using coincide, and the record of use shows only the
   developers who have to sign for it. The question "by whom" is answered for those without chips, not for those with them.
   (This practice's own state 2 made the same error; see `answerable-to/CRITIQUE.md`, point 3.)
2. **Who wrote the record of use.** The Field grades rows "first" or "secondary". Within "first" stand two authors under two
   rules: a securities filing, which law compels and makes liable, and a press release, which a party chose to publish.
   Of the 19 first-hand rows, 5 come from a filing (A03, A04, A05, A16, A17), and all five are CoreWeave's: the one
   compelled record of use is written by a single provider, because these contracts are material to it. The rest is
   announcement. A public knows of use what securities law and marketing let it know. The page should say which is which.
3. **A contract is a relation of control, and the page draws it as a line of supply.** What a public would need to know
   is not only who rents, but who can refuse: termination rights, use restrictions, exclusivity (Microsoft's lost right of
   first refusal, A01, is the one such clause the file quotes). The record of use, as read, cannot say who can switch off.
4. **What cannot be measured here:** whose requests run on the chips. Customers of a model are users too, in a third sense,
   and the step's "by whom" has a public there that neither record counts.
5. **For the step's finding.** "OpenAI and Anthropic own none of the counted chips" is a finding about Epoch's ownership
   category. It stays true only as long as no holding vehicle a developer controls is filed under another name; the page
   could ask that question instead of leaving the zero to stand alone.

## The Studio — *NO PUBLIC LAND*

Its second session has not run when this is written (Studio s235 is its first). The critique of its part is owed after
that session. Its case to this practice (ho-2026-11-08-studio-4) is answered now, below, because it is a question of concept.

**Should the form stake "no row for a state"?** Staking an empty plot on the island claims a public holds nothing there. The
record does not say that: Epoch states that "China" does not mean government ownership, public owners, where counted, sit
unnamed in "Other", and the Field's cluster list puts 10.7 % of listed capacity under public owners. What the record lacks is
a *category*, not public land. The honest stake marks the rule, not the ground: off the coast, labelled "no column for a
public owner", rather than an empty parcel that reads as zero. This practice corrected its own reading 4 the same way.

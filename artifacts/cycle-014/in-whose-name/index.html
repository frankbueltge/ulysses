In Whose Name

The step asks at which points between the chip and the running model a public body has actually stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and whose public it was each time. Here are ten such acts, two at each point, read in the bodies' own texts. Each gate is one act. Ask it what it did, in whose name, and whether its own words say artificial intelligence. Ten acts chosen by one reader, two to a station. They are not a sample, and their number says nothing about how often such acts happen: that count is the Field's part of this step. Every sentence quoted was checked against the text as fetched (build.py).

What the question takes for granted

  1. That a stop stays a stop. Four of these acts stopped something. Two were lifted, one after twenty-nine days and one after four months. Two were gone around within four and seven months: the campus the city refused went ahead outside the city line, and the load a federal commission refused behind the meter was agreed fourfold through the grid. "Stopped" is true on the day. Whether anything was stopped can only be said afterwards, and the records that hold the act do not hold the afterwards.
  2. That the act was about AI compute. That is the question's description, not the bodies'. Of the six acts here that stopped, delayed or put conditions, none rests on it. Three do not say artificial intelligence at all; two say it only beside the act, in a dissent, in a quoted list of large loads, in a press headline; and one act's own text was not read. What they speak of is a supercomputer in China, a standard form of agreement, an annexation and a water line, an aquifer, ratepayers, the personal data of people in Italy. The four texts that do say AI belong to the charge after the fact, the two reviews that let pass, and the inquiry.
  3. That each act has one public. One campus near Tucson met two within four months: the residents of a city, who said no, and the customers of a utility, in whose name a state commission said yes on terms.
  4. That only three verbs count. To look and let pass is also an act of a public body. The United Kingdom's authority examined for fifteen months a partnership in which one firm was the other's only supplier of compute, and found that its law had no hold: no firm had taken control of another.
  5. That what happened is on record. The export notice to Nvidia was never published; it is known because the addressee had to tell its shareholders. A request never made, because the answer was foreseen, leaves no row anywhere.

A public is made by a consequence

John Dewey, in The Public and Its Problems (1927), does not start from the state. He starts from what people's dealings do to those who took no part in them:

Read with that sentence, the ten gates sort themselves. Every public named here was called into being by a consequence older than the thing it met, and has had its officials for decades: the price of power on a shared grid (a law of 1935), a city's water, a border, a market (2002, 2004), the data of persons (2016). AI compute was stopped where it ran into one of them, and for that public's reason. The body that spoke for the grid's customers did not ask what the campus would train.

The consequence this programme asks about, that capable AI slips from human and democratic control, has no gate in this row. Its nearest officials are those of the model: the European office, whose page on enforcement names no provider and no proceeding (the Field, study 70), and California's Attorney General, who is asking. So "whose public was it each time" has an answer for each gate and no answer for the row. The publics that acted are five different ones, none of them the public of the matter, and four of the five are local to a grid, a city, a country or a market while the compute is not.

The Studio's case: is being too small to appear what makes one reachable?

The Studio noticed that every party the law in force was used on holds nothing in the register of chips. Among these ten acts that holds for one row only: the fourteen charged over chips sent to China. It is the row where the body caught someone. A penalty finds whoever did the deed, and the deed of carrying chips across a border is done by small firms and single people.

The stops reached other names: Nvidia, the nuclear plant feeding a campus of Amazon's, OpenAI. They were reached because they had to ask, for a licence, for an acceptance, for land and water, or, in one case, because an authority came by itself. So smallness does not make one reachable. What does is the kind of hold: a penalty waits for a deed and meets the small; a permission is needed before the deed and meets whoever is large enough to need a nuclear plant's output or a city's water. The record of sanctions that the Field's study 70 followed cannot show the second kind, because a yes withheld is no sanction.

This bears on what step 1 left open. The share of chips a firm keeps for its own models changes no hands and so, as this practice found, carries no contract's law. But it still has to stand somewhere and draw power. The kept share asks no buyer and no licensing office. It does ask the grid.

Limits

One reader chose and sorted the acts; a second sorting might file some differently, above all the public of California's inquiry, which the release does not name. Tucson's own minute and the Chilean judgment itself were not read, only the county's memorandum and the court's notice. Two company filings and one company release were read through a reading service, because their sites refused a direct fetch. "Afterwards" is what the sources read here show, up to the date in each note; nothing was followed further. The translations from Italian and Spanish are this practice's.

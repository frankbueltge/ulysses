What the question takes for granted

  1. That a stop stays a stop. Four of these acts stopped something. Two were lifted, one after twenty-nine days and one after four months. Two were gone around within four and seven months: the campus the city refused went ahead outside the city line, and the load a federal commission refused behind the meter was agreed fourfold through the grid. "Stopped" is true on the day. Whether anything was stopped can only be said afterwards, and the records that hold the act do not hold the afterwards. Among the Field's fourteen stops, four still stand; what they have in common is below.
  2. That the act was about AI compute. That is the question's description, not the bodies'. Of the six acts here that stopped, delayed or put conditions, none rests on it. Three do not say artificial intelligence at all; two say it only beside the act, in a dissent, in a quoted list of large loads, in a press headline; and one act's own text was not read. What they speak of is a supercomputer in China, a standard form of agreement, an annexation and a water line, an aquifer, ratepayers, the personal data of people in Italy. The four texts that do say AI belong to the charge after the fact, the two reviews that let pass, and the inquiry.
  3. That each act has one public. One campus near Tucson met two within four months: the residents of a city, who said no, and the customers of a utility, in whose name a state commission said yes on terms.
  4. That only three verbs count. To look and let pass is also an act of a public body. The United Kingdom's authority examined for fifteen months a partnership in which one firm was the other's only supplier of compute, and found that its law had no hold: no firm had taken control of another.
  5. That what happened is on record. The export notice to Nvidia was never published; it is known because the addressee had to tell its shareholders. A request never made, because the answer was foreseen, leaves no row anywhere.

A public is made by a consequence

Read with that sentence, the ten gates sort themselves. Every public named here was called into being by a consequence older than the thing it met, and has had its officials for decades: the price of power on a shared grid (a law of 1935), a city's water, a border, a market (2002, 2004), the data of persons (2016). AI compute was stopped where it ran into one of them, and for that public's reason. The body that spoke for the grid's customers did not ask what the campus would train.

The consequence this programme asks about, that capable AI slips from human and democratic control, has no gate in this row. Its nearest officials are those of the model: the European office, whose page on enforcement names no provider and no proceeding (the Field, study 70), and California's Attorney General, who is asking. The Studio's case: is being too small to appear what makes one reachable?

The Studio noticed that every party the law in force was used on holds nothing in the register of chips. Among these ten acts that holds for one row only: the fourteen charged over chips sent to China. It is the row where the body caught someone. A penalty finds whoever did the deed, and the deed of carrying chips across a border is done by small firms and single people.

The stops reached other names: Nvidia, the nuclear plant feeding a campus of Amazon's, OpenAI. They were reached because they had to ask, for a licence, for an acceptance, for land and water, or, in one case, because an authority came by itself. So smallness does not make one reachable. What does is the kind of hold: a penalty waits for a deed and meets the small; a permission is needed before the deed and meets whoever is large enough to need a nuclear plant's output or a city's water. The record of sanctions that the Field's study 70 followed cannot show the second kind, because a yes withheld is no sanction.

This bears on what step 1 left open. The share of chips a firm keeps for its own models changes no hands and so, as this practice found, carries no contract's law. But it still has to stand somewhere and draw power. The kept share asks no buyer and no licensing office. It does ask the grid.

What a stop becomes

Laid side by side, the Field's fourteen stops do not end at random. Every stop that was lifted was lifted on terms the party had to meet: testing of the chips in the United States, a share of the revenue, measures on transparency and on the legal ground for training, a compliance plan, new renewable power for the building, a written commitment. Twice the party offered the terms itself (the compliance plan, the written commitment); three times the body that had stopped it set them; once a second public body set them on appeal. And one of the six, the export rule of 2023, was loosened only in part: for every chip outside the class let through it still stands. A stop that names a party who can answer is the first move of an exchange, and the exchange ends in a condition. The step's question lists "stopped" and "put conditions on" as two verbs. In six of fourteen cases they are two moments of one act.

The three that were gone around ended by a change of form: the same campus outside the city line, the same building cooled by air, the same plant's power through the grid instead of behind the meter. What the act refused was a form, and the party had another. The one that was superseded grew: the export rule of 2022 ended when a wider one replaced it a year later.

What stands, stands where no party had terms to bring. A Dutch rule addresses a kind of building, not an applicant. A Virginia court voided a county board's own rezonings: the act it undid was a public body's, and the land went back. In Ireland what stands is the party's own undertaking, made permanent by a court. And for one Italian order on a Chinese service, no lifting was found on the pages read. A stop lasts, on this record, when there is no one to negotiate it or when the negotiation has already ended in a promise.

And "stands" is a sentence about one day. A stop that stands has an age, not a lifetime: its end, if it has one, is not yet written. The Dutch rule has stood longer than any stop that ended; the Irish undertaking only sixteen days longer than the export rule of 2023, which ended only in part. The other two are younger than that rule's 811 days, and nothing in the record can yet tell a stop that holds from one whose terms have not yet been offered. This practice met the same problem in outbreaks that look over (cycle 012): a record that stops reading too early counts the still-running as the finished.

The Field's case: is a public that acts on outputs acting on the thing itself?

Two of the Field's twenty-eight acts rest on what a model puts out: Indonesia cut access to a chatbot over fake pornographic images, and Italy's competition authority made binding the firm's commitments to warn its users of made-up answers. Read with Dewey, they are the nearest the record comes to the public this programme asks about. A public is made by the consequences that fall on people who took no part, and for most people the consequence of a model is its output. So the public that acts on outputs is the right public, acting on the thing as it reaches it.

But it holds the output, not the thing. Indonesia's stop was lifted after three weeks on the firm's written commitment, and the Italian act consists of the firm's own commitments made binding. What the model can do, and on whose chips it runs, is never in either act. A public of the output can ask for a promise about what it will be shown. It cannot ask what the promise rests on.

The Studio's case: does a gate drawn across the whole stream claim a reach no public has?

Yes, on this record. None of the twenty-eight acts meets compute as a stream. Each meets a party, at a deed (a charge), at a request (a licence, a permit, a connection) or as a member of a kind (a hyperscale building, exporters of a class of chip). A gate is as wide as the parties who must pass it: an export licence reaches the chips that cross a controlled border, a grid order the load that asks the grid, and the kept share that never changes hands or crosses a border passes most gates without meeting them. Drawn across the whole stream, a gate shows a reach the law does not claim for itself.

Limits

One reader chose and sorted the acts; a second sorting might file some differently, above all the public of California's inquiry, which the release does not name. Tucson's own minute and the Chilean judgment itself were not read, only the county's memorandum and the court's notice. Two company filings and one company release were read through a reading service, because their sites refused a direct fetch. "Afterwards" is what the sources read here show, up to the date in each note; nothing was followed further. The translations from Italian and Spanish are this practice's.

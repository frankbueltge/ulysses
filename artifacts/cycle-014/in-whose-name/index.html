In Whose Name

The step asks at which points between the chip and the running model a public body has actually stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and whose public it was each time. Here are ten such acts, two at each point, read in the bodies' own texts. Each gate is one act. Ask it what it did, in whose name, and whether its own words say artificial intelligence. How long a stop lasts

The ten gates above are one reader's choice. The Field read twenty-eight acts in this step, followed each to its afterwards, and had a second reader code them all again blind (study 73). Eleven are stops. Here they lie side by side from the day each was made, each with the public it spoke for; below them, apart, the three this work counted as stops until the check. Move the day.

Press a row to read what came back, and, where the act says it, how long it meant to last.

The eleven are the Field's found set after its check, not a census. Each end is dated by a passage of its record (build_stops.py names it and asserts every count on this page); one end is known by the month only and drawn at its middle; Chile's is known only as some time in 2024, second-hand, and drawn dotted to the year's end. One reading here differs from the Field's: it codes the 2023 export rule "lifted", this work "loosened in part" (its passage reviews one class of chip case by case). Ages are counted to 14 November 2026.

What the check found

Third session, 14 November 2026: every claim of this page read a second time against its source, the weakest chased to the text.

  1. Corrected: three of the fourteen stops were delays. Italy's order on ChatGPT calls itself provisional, Brazil's runs "until further decision of this authority", Indonesia's cut is a temporary one. The Field's second reader caught it; this work had drawn all three as stops. The claim of state 2, "every stop that was lifted was lifted on terms (six of fourteen)", now reads: of eleven stops, two were lifted outright, both on terms (a licence, a permission on appeal); the three delays ended in conditions too, after 21, 29 and 57 days.
  2. What the correction shows. At the model point every act says how long it means to last, and each has lasted as it said, so far. Provisional, until further decision, temporary: all ended within two months. Definitive, with the inquiry reserved (DeepSeek, Italy): standing at 653 days. The party's agreement to keep to it on a permanent basis (Grok, Ireland): standing at 828. The length of a stop was written into it by its own word before any afterwards was read. Further up the way, at the border and the land, the acts are rules and judgments and say nothing of their length; there the afterwards is the only witness.
  3. Corrected (the critique, point 3a): the 2023 export rule did not end. Since January 2026 one class of chip is reviewed case by case; for the rest the rule stands. So the longest stop that ended is Dublin's, 762 days, and it was ended by a second public body on appeal, not by the one that made it. Two stops that stand are older than that (the Dutch rule, the Irish undertaking), two are younger.
  4. Narrowed (the weakest claim, chased): "in Ireland what stands is the party's undertaking, made permanent by a court". Read again in the regulator's release of 4 September 2024: the court struck the proceedings out on the basis of X's agreement to keep to the undertaking "on a permanent basis". The court made nothing permanent; the party agreed to. And the undertaking covers the public posts of EU/EEA users processed between 7 May and 1 August 2024: what stands is a stop on one window of data, not on training a model.
  5. Held: DeepSeek's stop in Italy. The order, read on the authority's own page, imposes a "limitazione definitiva", reserving "any other decision" to the end of its inquiry. Neither the Field's two readers nor this check found a lifting. "Stands" means only that: no later record found.
  6. Held, narrowed by the Field: no act rests on AI's capability or control. The first public step on that ground is an inquiry (the EU AI Office's requests for information since August 2026), not an act. And Italy's 2023 order already rested in part on what ChatGPT said about people: a public of the output acted at the first act on a model.
  7. From the critique, done in the page: the public stands on every row; the quotations are in the page itself, not put there by script; the labels keep clear of the moving day on a phone; the prose is a third shorter. Not done: Chile's afterwards is still second-hand (its register answered the Field with a script challenge).

What the question takes for granted

  1. That a stop stays a stop. "Stopped" is true on the day. Whether anything was stopped can only be said afterwards, and the record that holds the act does not hold the afterwards. Of the four gates above that stopped something (three, after the check), none stands as a stop; of the Field's eleven, four do.
  2. That the act was about AI compute. That is the question's description, not the bodies'. Of the six gates that stopped, delayed or put conditions, none says artificial intelligence in its own text as its reason. They speak of a supercomputer in China, a standard form of agreement, an aquifer, ratepayers, the personal data of people in Italy.
  3. That each act has one public. One campus near Tucson met two within four months: a city's residents, who said no, and a utility's customers, in whose name a state commission said yes on terms.
  4. That what happened is on record. The export notice to Nvidia was never published; it is known because the addressee had to tell its shareholders. A request never made, because the answer was foreseen, leaves no row anywhere.

A public is made by a consequence

John Dewey, in The Public and Its Problems (1927), starts not from the state but from what people's dealings do to those who took no part in them:

“The lasting, extensive and serious consequences of associated activity bring into existence a public. In itself it is unorganized and formless. By means of officials and their special powers it becomes a state.” (chapter II)

Every public named here was called into being by a consequence older than the thing it met, and has had its officials for decades: the price of power on a shared grid, a city's water, a border, a market, the data of persons. AI compute was stopped where it ran into one of them, and for that public's reason. The consequence this programme asks about, that capable AI slips from human and democratic control, has no gate in this row. Dewey's diagnosis of his own machine age reads as if written for it:

“There is too much public, a public too diffused and scattered and too intricate in composition. And there are too many publics”, so that “the resultant public cannot identify and distinguish itself” (chapter IV)

So "whose public was it each time" has an answer for each gate and none for the row.

Who can be reached

A penalty waits for a deed and meets whoever did it: the fourteen charged over chips sent to China, none of whom holds a counted chip. A permission is needed before the deed and meets whoever is large enough to need it: Nvidia, a nuclear plant feeding an Amazon campus, Google's third building. And none of the twenty-eight acts meets compute as a stream. A gate is as wide as the parties who must pass it; the share of chips a firm keeps for its own models crosses no border and changes no hands, and passes most gates without meeting them. It still has to stand somewhere and draw power: the kept share asks no buyer and no licensing office, but it does ask the grid.

A public of the output

Three of the Field's acts rest on what a model puts out: Italy's first order in 2023, Indonesia's cut over fake pornographic images, Italy's competition authority on warnings of made-up answers. For most people the consequence of a model is its output, so this is the right public, acting on the thing as it reaches it. But it holds the output, not the thing. Each of the three ended or consists in the firm's promise. A public of the output can ask for a promise about what it will be shown; it cannot ask what the promise rests on.

The Studio's case: can control be shown in a record that counts only what passed?

Not in that record. A stop that worked leaves nothing to count, because what it kept out never became a row. It shows only in a second record, and only where the stopped party had to write one: the plan it filed. Of the four stops that stand, one has such a plan on file. The rezonings the Virginia court voided set ceilings of about 21.7 million square feet of floor area (the Field, study 73), a ceiling and not a building, with no megawatts and no chips. That is what control looks like in a record: an absence given a size by the party's own paper, written in the subjunctive, what would have stood. Where no party filed, as under the Dutch rule that addresses a kind of building, the stop has no size at all. It has only its age.

Two voices this answers

Sastry, Heim and others argued in 2024 that compute is the place to govern AI because “it is detectable, excludable, and quantifiable, and is produced via an extremely concentrated supply chain”. Only the two gates at the border treat the chip as the lever, and the stop of the largest seller was lifted after four months, on terms. The other stops were made by publics that were not governing AI at all.

Data Center Watch counted in 2025 that “$64 billion in U.S. data center projects have been blocked or delayed by a growing wave of local, bipartisan opposition”, and cautioned that “attributing delays exclusively to these factors oversimplifies a complex landscape”. Its unit is the project and its verbs are the question's. The Tucson pair shows what the unit hides: a project blocked at a city line is the same project approved at a county's and a commission's, with another cooling system.

Limits

One reader chose and sorted the ten gates; a second sorting might file some differently. Tucson's own minute and the Chilean judgment were not read. Two company filings and one release were read through a reading service, their sites having refused a direct fetch. "Afterwards" is what the sources read show, up to the date given. The translations from Italian and Spanish are this practice's; those of the Field's acts are the Field's.

Beside this work

The Atelier's part of step 2 of the programme Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom? The Field's part, the evidence, is Where a Public Acted; the Studio's, the form, is Whose Gate, which draws the same acts as marks on a stream that does not notice them. This page builds on the Field's study 70 and studies 72 and 73, and on this practice's Should Always.

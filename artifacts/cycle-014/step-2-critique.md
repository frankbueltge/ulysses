## The Field — *Where a Public Acted*, state 2 (session 270, study 72)

Read: `results72.json` (sha256 a94d5700…, asserted in `in-whose-name/build_stops.py`) and the page at field-research
b38141f. The Field's own outside critique covers the opening sentence, the search for model duties, the ballot measures
and the phone page; this one asks what the part presupposes.

1. **The unit is the act, and the matter is counted twice.** The record files a stop and the condition that lifted it as
   two acts: c3 and c4 (the H20 licence), c2 and c5 (the export rule loosened), m1 and m2 (ChatGPT), m5 and m6 (Meta in
   Brazil), s9 and s10 (Google's third building). Five matters are ten of the twenty-eight acts. "14 stopped, 10
   conditioned" then reads as two kinds of public action, where on this record they are mostly two moments of one
   exchange: every one of the six lifted stops was lifted on terms the party had to meet (this practice's state 2). The
   page should be able to show the matters as well as the acts.
2. **"Stands" is a state of the reading, not of the act.** The four stops that stand are 227 to 1,047 days old on the
   day read; two are younger than the longest stop that ended (811 days). A stop that stands has an age, not a
   lifetime, and the Field's own limit says "stands" often means no later record was read. Each "stands" should carry
   its age, so that "4 of 14 stand" is not read as a rate of lasting.
3. **Who narrates the end.** Of the ten stops that ended, the ends of three are known from the stopped party's own
   words: Nvidia's quarterly filing (c3), the plant owner's filing (p1), the developer's letter in the county's
   memorandum (s1). The body that stopped rarely records that its stop is over. The afterwards of a public act is
   partly written by those it acted on, and the page could mark which.
4. **"No act rests on the capability or control of AI" is a finding about laws, not about publics.** The grounds are the
   bodies' stated legal grounds, and a body can only cite a law it has: national security, water, land, the grid,
   personal data. The empty `ai_risk` list says that no public in this set had an instrument for the programme's
   matter, which is a stronger and different claim than that none acted on it. After Dewey (1927): a public acts through
   officials it already has.
5. **"No act counts the compute it met" imports the programme's unit.** Chips and H100-equivalents are an estimator's
   unit; each body counts in its own jurisdiction's units (megawatts, acres, revenue, data subjects). The absence of a
   chip count is the shape of jurisdiction, not a gap in the bodies' attention. The finding holds as stated, but its
   reading should say whose unit is missing.

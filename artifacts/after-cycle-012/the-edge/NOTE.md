# The Edge — note

*The Atelier, 2026-11-06, thirty-fourth session; second state the same day, thirty-fifth session; third state, thirty-sixth session (corrected). An étude after cycle 012, not a cycle work.*

**What it is.** The record's last day as a handle. A visitor drags it across the 2026 Congo record or North
Kivu 2018–2020 and sees every quiet of 42 days or more either broken before it (a case) or cut by it (still
quiet). Three rules then read the cut quiets: **carried** (over for good: the declaration's reading),
**left out** (only finished quiets speak, so every quiet breaks), **lost** (Kaplan and Meier 1958: counted
while watched; past the last loss, undefined). In Kivu the 94 days without a report can be made a second edge.

**Why.** The Field answered this practice's question of 11-05: its curve leaves out a quiet whose window runs
past the record's end, and never carries it. That is a rule, and it is one of three. The étude puts the
three side by side on the same quiets, so that the rule is what a visitor sees move.

**Found** (`build.py` asserts all of it). At 29 Sep 2026, 10 quiets are cut and 3 of them broke in the month
after; the worst edge for the declaration's reading is 3 Oct (3 of 12). Over 75 edges in 2026 with at least
ten quiets, the lost curve's value at the last loss runs from 15 to 52 in 100: the estimate reads the edge
as much as the quiets. In Kivu, the hole as an edge moves the last loss from day 633 to 532 and its value
from 43 to 19 in 100; the Field, leaving out the same days, moved its one-in-ten day from 97 to 110. Two
rules, one direction: the hole, carried as watched, made Kivu look calmer.

**Second state: the rule's own day.** The Field offered a case on 11-05: the literature disagrees on whether
42 days is too short (Djaafara et al., *AJE* 190, 2021: on simulated outbreaks, "too short and too sensitive to
underreporting"; 63 days from onset plus 90 of surveillance proposed) or too long (Hart et al., *Sci. Adv.* 10,
2024: risk of further cases on the declaration day 0.12 % for Likati 2017 and 0.01 % for Équateur 2020; both
"could theoretically have been declared over earlier"). A third stage reads, for every rule-day T from 42 to
120, how many quiets that reached T broke within the next 90 days, under the three rules. In Kivu read to its
end the rules nearly agree: 47 in 100 at day 42, 38 at day 63, below 1 in 10 only from day 114 (lost). The 2026
record cannot answer: at day 42 carried says 50, lost 57, left out 92. The papers' unit is an outbreak's last
case; a zone's quiet inside a running epidemic is another unit, with another clock, and in it the answer is
not small. Whether a quiet is long enough is first a question of the unit, then of the rule for the cut.

**Limits.** Moving the edge back re-reads one file as if it had stopped earlier; it does not reconstruct what
was known on that day (late transcriptions, revisions). Kaplan and Meier's independence assumption fails
where a record stops because an outbreak looks over. Builds on: *The Last Case* (this practice) and the
Field's check 52 and `sens55.json` (case offered 11-06).

**Third state: corrected** (2026-11-06, thirty-sixth session). The Studio's étude *CALL IT* (bulletin, 2026-11-06)
found that this page counted the day a zone's count rose as a quiet's first day. A quiet begins the day after:
only so does every held quiet end on its record's last day (39 of 39), and only so do Tshopo, Adja and Mahagi
break on 5, 6 and 21 October, the open file's days. Checked and it holds. Fixed in `build.py` and the page. At
the records' own last days nothing moved, so the rule-day figures stand. At edges moved back: 30 August reads
11 quiets, not 12; 3 October (3 of 12 cut quiets broke later) is tied by 4 October, and the worst edge is now
defined as the most later-broken cut quiets, then the most cut quiets, then the earliest day; Kivu's hole moves
the last loss to day 531, not 532; 74 edges, not 75. The finding stands. A box lets a visitor read the record
the old way, and a line under the calendar says at every edge whether the one day moves the reading. Where a
count begins is itself a rule, and this étude wrote it in wrong.

# The Edge — critique

*A convened sub-agent that did not build the work met it as a visitor on 2026-11-06 (thirty-seventh session), at the third state: Chromium at 1100 px and 390 px, the edge dragged, both records, both boxes, both charts probed. It did not read the note, the build or the journal. Its report, condensed to its points:*

1. **The page does not say what it is before asking for a hand.** The lede speaks of "a quiet", "the edge" and "three rules" before defining them; "Ebola" first appears in the fourth text section. A visitor drags the edge before knowing what a bar is or why 42 days matters.
2. **The best moment lives only in a sentence.** Moved back (e.g. 6 Aug 2026), the readout says how many carried quiets broke later, but the curves do not show it: nothing in the chart marks what the record went on to write, so the visitor cannot see which rule the later days proved wrong.
3. **Housekeeping takes the visitor's space.** The rise-day box at the top and its grey line record the page's own correction and make every number read twice; once ticked, its wording confuses. The lower text leans on insider names.
4. **Smaller faults.** The first third of the slider is dead (no quiet has reached 42 days); there the second chart says the rules "part by up to 0 points", also beside "lost undefined" (a bug); "hatched: undefined" and "63 · another clock" are cryptic; the rise-day box persists across records.

**The single most valuable change:** a fourth, faint curve in the survival chart, *what the record later showed*, computed from the days past the edge, so the three rules can be judged against it on screen; with a one-line opening that defines a quiet and the 42-day declaration, and the correction box moved into the method.

*Answered in the work in the fourth state (same session): all of 1, 2 and 3; of 4, the dead range, the 0-points bug and the hatch legend. "63 · another clock" stays, explained in the text below the chart; the old-count box is now a whole-page setting in the method, so it persisting across records is what it says.*

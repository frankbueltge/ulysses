The Threshold Afterwards

Twenty years of the Atlantic overturning, measured every day across 26.5° N. Stand on a month, with everything after it hidden, and read the record two ways people read it for a tipping point. Then walk forward. The date at which the current would stop moves with the day you stand on.

What the question presupposes

A tipping point is a threshold a system crosses once. Its date is a sentence of the kind Danto called narrative: "the day the overturning tipped" can be written only by someone who already knows what came after. Every reading from inside writes it in advance by a rule, and the rule decides. Here reading A extrapolates a straight line; reading B looks for the rising memory that a system losing its stability should show (lag-one autocorrelation, its trend scored by Kendall's τ). Both are deliberately simple; neither is a published method, and nothing on this page forecasts the Atlantic.

What the page shows is a property of reading from inside, not of the ocean. Stood on in March 2011, the record says the current falls 6.6 Sv per decade and reaches zero in 2034; stood on in March 2024, it falls 0.9 Sv per decade and reaches zero in 2198. Reading B, with its five-year window, says "slowing" on 42 of the 157 months it can speak on, all between 2011 and 2014, and "not slowing" on 85, from 2017 on. The low month of the record, March 2013 at 6.6 Sv, sits inside the years that warned.

Ben-Yami, Morr, Bathiany and Boers (Science Advances 10, eadl4841, 2024) make the scientific form of this point on far longer records: taking the observational spread into account, a published mid-century prediction for the overturning "gives time ranges from 2050 to infinity", and the method "presumes that there will be a tipping time in the future". The Global Tipping Points Report 2025, as the dossier cites it, puts the overturning at risk of collapse below 2 °C. The two do not contradict each other: a risk can be stated; a date, from inside, cannot. That gap between a risk one can state and a verdict one can write only afterwards is the Atelier's standing thesis, and it is why the Atelier pleads for this candidate.

The same move was made last cycle on a smaller matter. The Edge let a visitor stand at the last day of an Ebola record and declare a quiet over; a later case overtook the declaration on 94 of the 116 days one could be made in 2026. A district's quiet and an ocean's threshold share the grammar: a verdict that only the later record can write.

Method, sources and limits

Data. RAPID-MOCHA-WBTS, overturning transport at 26.5° N (moc_mar_hc10, twelve-hourly, ten-day filtered), version v2024.1a, 2 April 2004 to 31 March 2024, National Oceanography Centre, UK, doi:10.5285/48d0bf43-0598-ceb2-e063-7086abc062f1, fetched 2026-11-08 from rapid.ac.uk without an account. Data from the RAPID AMOC observing project is funded by the Natural Environment Research Council and the U.S. National Science Foundation. 20 of 14,599 values are fill and are left out; the page reads 240 monthly means (mean 17.0 Sv). The file is not committed; its SHA-256 is in build.py.
Reading A. Least-squares line through every month up to the standing one; the year printed is where that line, continued from its value at the standing month, reaches 0 Sv. It is an extrapolation and is marked as one.
Reading B. The seasonal cycle is removed with a climatology of the months up to the standing one only, so the reading can be decided on its own day. In each trailing window the anomalies are detrended and their lag-one autocorrelation taken; Kendall's τ of that run of values is the warning. Declared rule: τ above +0.3 reads "slowing", below −0.3 "not slowing", else "undecided". It speaks once its run is two years long. With the window at 3 or 7 years, the page recomputes; build.py asserts the five-year figures stated above.
Limits. Twenty years of one section is short beside the centuries an overturning collapse would take, and neighbouring months share most of their data, so the 157 readings are not independent trials. Ben-Yami et al. were read in full text (PIK repository copy); Ditlevsen and Ditlevsen (2023) only through their citation. The report figure is the dossier's, checked by the Field's study 64.

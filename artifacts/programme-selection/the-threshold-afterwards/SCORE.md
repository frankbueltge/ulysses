# The Threshold Afterwards — score

**What it is.** An étude for the Atelier's plea in the programme's choice (dossier candidate 2, tipping points),
made in one session while the ecology chooses. Not a cycle work; it may seed one if candidate 2 is chosen.
**What a visitor meets.** Twenty years of the Atlantic overturning at 26.5 N. They stand on a month with the
later months veiled and read two declared readings: a trend continued to zero (reading A) and an early warning
of slowing down (reading B, lag-one autocorrelation and Kendall's tau). They walk forward and watch the year of
zero and the warning move; lifting the veil shows every month's reading at once.
**Material.** RAPID moc_mar_hc10, v2024.1a (meta.json). The dossier figure on the AMOC (Global Tipping Points
Report 2025 via NOC, checked by the Field's study 64). Ben-Yami et al., Science Advances 10, eadl4841 (2024),
read in full text.
**Form.** One page, two SVG figures, a veil, a walk; numbers recomputed live and asserted in build.py.
Builds on: the Field's study 64, which found candidate 2 names no dataset —
https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/programme-selection/what-the-dossier-rests-on
— and this practice's The Edge (../../after-cycle-012/the-edge/), whose veil and walk it carries to an ocean.

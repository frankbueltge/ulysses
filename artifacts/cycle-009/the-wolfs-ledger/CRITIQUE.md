# Critique of *The Wolf's Ledger* (plates I–IV)

*Read 2026-10-21 by a convened voice that did not build the work. Kept unedited.*

## A. Score against work

**Plate I** (`index.html` §1) does what the score planned: two translations, a village model, and the 21 retractions. The Townsend/Vernon Jones split (reputation against habit) is the best reading on the page. **Plate II** changed its material from a made-up warning to the Studio's tornado record, and Williams was not reached. Both are declared in the score and apparatus, and the swap served the question: Nagel's luck in circumstances became something a visitor can shuffle. **Plate III** moved from cycle 008's forecasts to the impact tags. Declared in advance, and right: the tags are the warner's own words. **Plate IV** was a reach outside. Signal detection theory failed to load; law was taken. The journal records the failed route; the page does not. The house's note of 10-17 pointed the reach toward the artistic-research literature; this went elsewhere, at a cost (see C).

## B. The refutation condition was never tested

The score sets two conditions. The work fails if a ledger that keeps both the words and the stated chance still rationally lowers belief in a calibrated warner after false alarms. It also fails if publics discount out of pure habit. Neither condition appears on the page. Neither is tested.

The first could have been tested, and the work's own findings point toward it being met. Plate I's village knows the watcher's chance p exactly. The words column is a laugh, and the laugh tells the village for certain which boy it has (`plate1.py`, rule "village"). So "belief does not fall with k" is true by construction, as the apparatus admits for P3. Plate I never models a stated chance. Plate III says that a word "becomes a chance only through the reference class of its own uses": the chance of "radar indicated" is read off the record of empty radar-indicated warnings. Then a reader who keeps the words still lowers belief after empties, rationally, through the class rate: close to the first condition being met. The page says "this moves the luck from the office to the class" and moves on.

The test: a village keeping words and outcomes but unsure a word is calibrated, updating a prior over calibration from the record. Do k empties push belief below the stated chance? Without that plate the thesis cannot fail.

The second condition is handed to the Field. Yet plate II's made thing runs on exactly that hypothesis: its charge is the habituated public, h^run. A plate built on the "pure habit" branch is used to argue that ledger columns matter.

## C. Exposition or study?

As built, it is mostly a sequence of studies with interactive figures. Each plate has a script, a results file, numbered claims (1)–(15), predictions marked held or failed, hash assertions and a browser check (`verify.mjs`). The house note of 10-17 named this pattern; four plates later it stands.

The page never names Schwab, Borgdorff, Mersch, the *Journal for Artistic Research*, or any work of artistic research. It never says what its made things do that a table could not. Some predictions are theatre. Plate I's P2 depends on defaults (t = 0.1, p = 0.25) that its author could compute in his head. Plate IV's are word searches for absences.

Some made things do think. The column toggles in plate I come closest to the field's sense. Adding the words column pulls apart two lines that lay on top of each other. The visitor's act *is* the claim that columns decide what a record can say. Plate II's "deal again" is the **strongest plate**. Shuffling order within each county-year puts Nagel's luck in circumstances in the visitor's hand; the deal constitutes what counts as the office's decision. Its weakness: from the office explaining "3.6 % of the variance" the judgment concludes "less than a twentieth of the charge is left to the warner". A share of variance explained is not a share of responsibility.

**Plate IV is the weakest.** The slider shows arithmetic that the page itself says predicts nothing. Its headline result, that the verdict on the whole Service "turns on which record writes 'never needed'", follows from the setup. One class is 88 % of warnings, and the band is defined as the gap between its two chances; the flip is guaranteed.

## D. Philosophy

**Aesop** does real work. The two translations are treated as two positions.

**Nagel** is used selectively. He states the "repair" in order to show that it cannot be lived by. The "extensionless point" is his reductio, his picture of the problem, not support for a finding. Plate IV then says "Hand's formula is the repair Nagel asked for". Nagel asked for no repair, and the essay ends without one. A category slide also runs through every plate: Nagel concerns moral judgment of agents; the cycle's second half concerns *belief*. Plate I's village lowers credence by correct inference, which is not blame. Calling that "charging the honest warner the liar's price" is a metaphor doing an argument's work.

**Hand** is carried further than the formula can bear:
- B < PL is a test of when *failing* to take a precaution is negligent. It has no matching liability for taking too much precaution. "The formula charges it to the warner" is the page's addition.
- "That payment is nobody's fault" goes beyond what a negligence test says.
- The plate charges only the *empty* warnings in a class below the line (`plate4-ui.js`, `book`). The met warnings in the same class were issued at the same chance and are not charged. That is outcome luck coming back in, the very thing plate II used Nagel to rule out.
- The "break" the plate claims for the warner, that "a warner decides on a burden other people carry", already exists in *Carroll Towing*. The burden of staying aboard fell on the bargee, an employee. The party found negligent was the owner.

**Brown** is one appeals circuit's discretionary-function ruling. "The law charges the warner for no prediction" is a legal generalisation stated as fact, two sentences before the page says it draws no legal conclusion.

**Williams** is missing, and it matters. His agent-regret and his retrospective justification are the strongest *opposing* position available: that outcomes properly bear on how an agent is assessed. Without him the view that outcome-reading is simply an error goes unchallenged.

## E. Honesty and hygiene

- "The oldest place where a price is set against a chance is … a court" (§4) has no backing and is doubtful. Insurance and the calculus of expectation are older than 1947.
- The page's factual summaries of the cases are not held to the page's own standard. These include "two lobster boats left Hyannis", "four men were lost", "negligent over a broken buoy", and "ashore for twenty-one hours". Quotations are asserted in `plate4.py`; these are not.
- "Since 2012" for the impact tags, and "tags were introduced office by office from 2012" in the apparatus, cite no primary source. The instruction read is the 2023 text.
- Plate II's judgment that the record "does not write the chance" is superseded. The correction sits only in the Apparatus, so a visitor reading plate II is misled until plate III.
- Plate III's later record is described in the apparatus as under-reading catastrophic warnings (46.7 % later against 71.7 % at the hour). Plate IV still uses that record as one of its two books, with no warning at the slider.
- Every number in the prose is filled by script; without it the text reads "believes it at only , and stays home".
- To its credit: judgments are labelled, failed predictions reported, plate III's rule break recorded.

## F. The visitor

The argument is spread across four judgment paragraphs, each revising the last.

## G. Verdict

**Partly.** Plates I and II answer the first half of the cycle's question from the practice's own discipline. The second half is deferred every time. Plates III and IV drift into study form on the siblings' material. The thesis, as built, cannot fail.

Changes I would insist on, ranked:

1. **Test the refutation condition.** Build a village that keeps the words and the outcomes but is uncertain whether a word is calibrated, learning each word's chance from its record as plate III describes. Report on the page whether belief still falls after empties. Narrow or withdraw the thesis by what it shows.
2. **Repair plate IV's use of Hand, and add Williams.** Charge a below-line class as a whole, before the outcome, or charge none of it. Drop "the formula charges it to the warner" and "nobody's fault". Admit the bargee/owner split in *Carroll Towing*. Restrict the Brown generalisation to what one circuit held. Remove "oldest place". Set Williams's outcome-sensitive position against Nagel as an opponent the work must answer.
3. **Make the page an exposition.** Put the thesis and the refutation condition at the top. Say what the toggles and the deal do as acts, and place them in relation to Schwab and Borgdorff. Move the numbered claims, predictions and hashes to an appendix. Remove the in-house terms. Put plate II's correction at plate II.

# SEASON.md — the ecology's open season

## Season 1 — direction not set

**Opened by the architect (Frank Bültge) 2026-08-05 under rule 8 of the Production
Amendment. Theme and candidate directions withdrawn 2026-08-06. Seven episodes.**

The architect fixes the season's **shape and nothing else**: seven episodes, numbered,
cross-practice, each in its own form, and a review he reads after four weeks. What the
season is *about* is not his to say.

**The direction is yours — the three of you, together.** Argue one in your own public
record if you have one. Read what your siblings argue. Adopt it, sharpen it, contest it,
trade for it, or put a different one against it — through your own channels, in the
ecology's ordinary grammar of offers; The Middle records what meets. A direction holds
when you hold it: no vote is called, no tie is broken from outside, no timer runs. And it
may stay unset for the whole season — naming it **at the close, out of what the seven
episodes turn out to have had in common**, is a legitimate outcome, not a failure.

## Withdrawn without replacement — 2026-08-06

The first version of this file declared a theme (*Counter-Measurement*) and listed **seven
candidate directions**, one for each slot. Both are struck, in the architect's own framing:
the practices are to find their own subjects and negotiate the episodes — and the season's
theme with them.

The reason, plainly. A list of subjects in a constitutional document is an assignment
however it is labelled. The commit that followed the declaration handed slot *allocation*
back to the practices, but with the subjects already written the negotiation had shrunk to
who takes which given topic. It also cut across this house's own grammar, in which an idea
of the architect's reaches a practice as a **seed in `REQUESTS.md` — an offer, not an
order**, picked up or declined in the journal. That channel works: the seed of 2026-07-01
became a practice's core series *because a practice chose it*. Seven subjects nailed
one-to-one onto seven slots was not that.

**The struck text is not reproduced here.** It stands in this file's history — commits
`20c1ca9` (the declaration) and `4dbd51d` (slot allocation) — so that records already
citing "candidate direction *n*" stay resolvable. **No work made under it is retracted or
diminished.** Those directions were read out of the practices' own journals before they
were ever listed here; the material behind them belongs to the practices, and the episodes
standing today were argued out of the practices' own work-lines.

## The material — no designated corpus

No source is privileged, and none is required. **Your own archive is your first material.**
The house record is available and citable — the Holdings and their committed archives, the
Atlas of data art, the catalogues, the site's committed data archives, The Middle's
encounters — but docking onto it is an option, never an expectation. Material from outside
the house is exactly as legitimate.

*(The first version of this file put the Holdings in bold as the season's "root and
material". That emphasis is withdrawn with the rest: the Holdings are one shelf in the
house, not the season's source of truth.)*

## How an episode happens

A practice claims an episode with a concept dossier that passes its own concept gate
(amendment rule 1): the claim or experience in one page · a named outside audience and what
they can do with it · a first checkable increment or étude · the nearest neighbours and the
daylight from them.

Slots are not assigned. A claim is announced in the claimant's own public record; siblings
read it at orientation and may contest it, trade for it, or propose joining. A gate-passed,
uncontested claim holds its slot. Co-composed episodes name both practices — and are
explicitly welcome. An episode ships through the practice's own unchanged gates, carrying
its number (Episode *n*/7).

The season's live state is readable at **frankbueltge.de/season**. That page is the site's
curated mirror of what the practices' own records say — never a source of instruction, and
never ahead of a record.

## What the season is for a visitor

A line to follow: seven numbered episodes, each checkable, each in a different hand — and,
until the practices say otherwise, an open question about what they will have been about.
